# LangHarness, November 2026: what was built, when, and what is still unverified

Times are UTC. "Unverified" means the owner has not yet used it in the live app; the sandbox checks and the tests were run in the cloud.

## 2 November: Quiet tool redesign (branch `feat/quiet-tool-redesign`, not merged)

- **Owner changes after the first pass (the mockups no longer bind where they conflict):** Today has no strip of seven icons and no legend; it is the plain plan, a list with each task's word label, minutes and state, and the next task as one large card with one Start (Enter starts it). Tasks that need a tutor chat are left out of the plan while no AI key is set, so Review and Today show no "Needs an AI key" item. The rail (and, on a phone, the end of Today) holds real charts: XP per day against the goal, a five-week streak calendar, level progress with the XP to the next level, and minutes by strand. The same charts open the Progress page. An empty week or a new learner gets a sentence in place of the chart. Read has no "line by line" (Veta po vete) mode: only the episode, tap-to-gloss and its questions.
- **Read, reworked:** the episode is one continuous text (21 px on a desktop, 19 px on a phone, 1.6 line height, about 65 characters a line) with tap-to-gloss; the sentence-by-sentence controls, the progress percent and the saved-sentence button are gone. A final full stop or a closing quote stays with the word before it (words and their punctuation are grouped so a line never breaks between them). The bar holds Play all, Translation on or off, and Questions. The header is close, title, Library and text size. New words are plain lines under the text. The library is English: one focal card with one "Read today's episode", a plain list below, level and status filters only from six stories, search from eleven, defaults All; empty and no-match states use the shared component. Test episodes: four hand-made Robo episodes in `scripts/fixtures/robo-episodes.ts` (Slovak written by a model, every sentence listed with its English translation for a native check), written into a scratch profile by `scripts/seed-stories.ts`, which `npm run dev:sandbox -- --seed` now runs. The Words page and the credits line are English too; the credits line moved from Review to Settings, General, Credits.
- **Empty, blocked and failed states:** one component (`components/quiet/empty-state.tsx`): what the screen is for, why it is empty, what still works, ONE primary action and at most a text link, near the top of a normal page and in plain words (no model slot, cheap, strong, token). Read opens the library without a key, with a quiet line that new episodes need an AI key; Watch and Speak say what works without a key; the Listen, Say it faster, Read, Watch and Mistakes empty and error screens say why and what to do; the AI callout and the "Set up your models" buttons became "Add an AI key". Words got a clear-filters action. Not changed: the Settings AI models page keeps its own model names, and the Words and Read library pages keep their Slovak copy.
- **Icon-only controls:** each got a visible label (Add to Review / In Review, Select, Slowly, Library, Videos, Repeat, Weak spot, Tutor, Goal, Priority, Earlier, Later); the plus icons on Learn a topic and Practice a topic were removed because the rows are already labeled.
- **Theme flash and font swap:** the saved Day or Night is now in the first HTML: a `lh-theme` cookie is read on the server (`app/layout.tsx`) into `data-theme` and the `dark` class on `<html>` and into the status-bar color, so nothing is applied by a script or an effect after load. With no saved choice the system's own setting decides by CSS media query alone. Before, an explicit Day on a dark system showed Night for about 1.8 s, and an explicit Night on a light system stayed Day until hydration (slow 3G-like throttle in headless Edge, frame by frame). Geist is preloaded (the code font no longer is) with `display: optional` and the size-matched fallback; layout shift on a cold Review load fell from 0.039 to 0.002 and on a warm reload from 0.103 to 0.021 (Review holds the space of its late card, header and buttons). The Electron window remembers the last theme in `userData/theme.json` so its background and title bar are right from the first frame. A theme saved by the earlier build in localStorage moves to the cookie on first load (one flash, once). There is no inline script: React warns when it re-renders one, and the cookie makes it unnecessary.
- **Log:** a Log item in the sidebar (route `/log`) and a compact "Today so far" on Today. Each day lists what was done with its time: lessons, Blitz, Mix-ups, Listen, Speak and Say it faster rounds ("10 of 12 right, 3 min"), Review sittings (answers 20 minutes apart are one sitting), the rules met, words added to Review (cards minted that day), mistakes (wrong first tries from lessons and Review), minutes by strand (measured, with the part counted from the plan said so) and the day's counts without a time (episodes read, videos watched). A day switcher keeps past days readable. Built only from the lesson log, the review log and cards, and the day record; no invented entries, and an empty day says so. The assembly (`src/core/daylog.ts`) is adapted from the Notebook design's page builder (`src/core/notebook.ts` on `notebook-redesign`), with Review sittings, minted words and strand minutes added; tests in `tests/daylog.test.ts`. The page reads on the browser's clock through `/api/daylog`, because the server's zone can differ and then "today" is the wrong day.
- **Marked words:** the tinted rounded box with a curved underline behind a cloze word is gone everywhere (Review, lessons, Exam, Listen, Read, the rule note and word pairs). Blanks are straight writing lines, a right or marked word is color and weight with a straight underline, word pairs and hint chips are plain text. Frosted-glass surfaces became opaque and the small all-caps labels became sentence case.
- **Sidebar bug:** the sidebar vanished at every width. My first pass made it start closed and kept a closed choice in a cookie and localStorage, and Today dropped the only button that reopened it. Now the sidebar is a permanent column on any window of 768 px and up (the Electron auto-close threshold is 900 px), the old cookie is ignored, and the close button shows only inside the phone drawer, which has a menu button on Today and Review.
- **What:** every route restyled in the Quiet tool look from the mockups in `r3-3-quiet`. Decision and rules: E32 in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
- **Built from a mockup:** Today (desktop with the rail, phone with the top bar), the Review card (typing, verdict, rating, the collapsed last rep, the rail "in progress" row), the jump bar and its palette, Speak with and without an AI key and My mistakes (content only, from `r2-hl-practice`), Listen and Read (content only, from `r2-hl-input`).
- **Night mode:** derived from Day (tokens listed in `app/globals.css`, `.dark`). The mockups have only Day.
- **Extended from the same components, no mockup:** the lesson question, verdict and end screens; Exam; Say it faster and Blitz; Listen end screen; Read library, reader and check; Watch; Speak scene, end screen and script player; Grammar atlas; Progress and "When can I…?"; Curriculum (now a calm list: stage rule heading, chapter strip, chapter cards; the mountain is gone and stages run A1 first); Milestones; Stamp book; the knowledge map (/map, canvas colors read from tokens); Words; Chat (new session, transcript, composer, question dock); Settings (every section; Mode is now System, Day or Night); first-run /start; /pair; dialogs, banners and the model picker; not-found.
- **Left out because the app cannot back it:** the Review sample sentence and rule note, the Estonian bridge card, "Why? Ask the tutor" on Review, "Add 3 minutes of Say it faster" and "Not today" on Today, the waveform and folded-text strip on Listen, the "Meanwhile, without AI" cards on Speak, mix-up swap cards and look-alike tiles on My mistakes. Today's weekly offer links to the strand's door instead.
- **Changed behavior:** keys 1 to 4 on Review now choose a rating and Enter or Continue confirms it (before, a key rated at once). The sidebar starts closed on a first visit. The factory heading weight is 600 (was 900) and the reading font is Geist, so `tests/settings*.ts` and the font-menu tests changed.
- **Not changed:** the Electron title bar overlay and window background still use the old dark ground; the plain-browser theme color follows Day and Night. Native selects remain in the ChatGPT and subscription connection panels, and native range, checkbox and select inputs in the map's settings.
- **Unverified:** Safari, a real phone, Night at 390 px on most screens, My mistakes with data, the Speak scene and Read gloss with an AI key, and Watch playing a video. One dev-only React warning ("script tag while rendering") appears on /curriculum.

## 2 November: pairing from a phone (dev origin)

- **Cause:** the phone loads the app's scripts from its tailnet name. Next's dev server refuses a dev resource from an origin it does not list in `allowedDevOrigins`, so every script got a 403 and the page never started. The pairing form then did nothing. The owner's dev log has those refusals for the phone's name. The guide said only hot reload was affected; that was wrong.
- **Fix:** `next.config.ts` lists the host in `LH_TAILNET_HOST`, read from `.env.local` (gitignored). Dev only, so a build never gets the name. The owner adds the line once and restarts `bun run desktop:dev`.
- **Checked:** the running dev server answers 403 to a script request from the tailnet name and passes a localhost one. Next's origin check lets the phone's script through with the variable set, and refuses it without. The remote-access and desktop-API tests pass (47 of 47); typecheck and lint pass.
- **Unverified:** the phone itself, after the restart: the pairing form, the code, and the cookie in Safari.

## 1 November: phone and iPad, reachability (second slice)

- **Route:** Tailscale Serve over the same loopback bind. The server does not listen on the network, even with remote access on, because Serve connects from this computer. Funnel is refused by design (E21). Options and sources: [research/remote-access.md](research/remote-access.md).
- **Switch:** Settings → Devices, off by default. Turning it off signs out every device, and the confirmation says so first.
- **Pairing:** a six-digit code, five minutes, five wrong tries, one code at a time. A paired device stays signed in for 180 days, is listed with its last use, and can be signed out on its own.
- **Gate:** while off, requests a proxy marked, and requests for another host, are refused. While on, the desktop window and paired devices pass; `/pair`, its one POST and the static assets stay public.
- **Recovery:** `npm run remote:off` turns the switch off and signs out every device.
- **Checked in the cloud, on a throwaway data folder:** 35 rule cases, 13 store cases (codes, tries, expiry, sessions, sign-out, turning off, an unreadable file), and a 16-step browser check against a sandbox server: the desktop switch and code, a phone pairing, the paired home screen, and turning it off again.
- **Unverified:** Tailscale and Serve on the owner's PC, and the headers Serve sends (`Host` and the `X-Forwarded-*` headers through Serve are not documented); the iPhone and iPad, Safari's cookie handling, and whether a Home Screen app shares Safari's cookies; the packaged desktop app with remote access on, whose port changes at each start, so it is not yet a fixed Serve target; dev hot reload through a tailnet name, which needs `allowedDevOrigins`; the PC itself, which needs a restart onto main. A Serve setup that sends no identity headers and keeps the Host as loopback would look like this computer; the guide tells the owner to turn the switch on first.
- **Test failures already on main:** 13 tests fail on main without this change: automatic-updates (6), desktop-payload (2), desktop-platform (1), fluency (1), rejected-examples (1), server-title-guard (1) and subscription-auth (1). The next section clears all but one of them.

## 1 November: mix-ups and My mistakes

- **Mix-ups** (`feat/learn-from-mistakes`, E24): a wrong first try that gives another word is kept per pair of words; families and an edit rule predict look-alikes, which are tested as a two-way choice before they are taught. New: the Mix-ups lesson, a Today card, the Strengthen task and a line in the tutor's learner block. English translations are no longer selectable for word lookup in lessons, review, exams, listen and fluency. Research: [research/mixups.md](research/mixups.md).
- **My mistakes** (`feat/learn-from-mistakes`, E25): `/mistakes` shows when your answers were last analyzed, a trend per unit, the words missed most, week by week, and the mix-ups, with an "Analyze now" button and an optional guide on the Cheap slot.
- **Acts on the plan:** a unit that is slipping becomes the Strengthen task; the page has "Do this next" buttons (Mix-ups lesson, a lesson on the weakest unit, add the missed words to review) and an "Add to review" button per word. A lesson renews a snapshot older than six hours.
- **AI, said plainly:** the guide now reads the latest wrong answers and returns patterns with why and a fix, guesses and a plan with buttons. Without a model or a key, the page shows a callout that says what is missing and what AI adds, and opens Settings. "Provider is not configured: anthropic" (a slot set with no key) is now "The anthropic key is missing. Add it in Settings → Keys…". Custom milestones use the same checks.
- **The personal lesson** (E26): the guide runs on the Strong slot and names what to practice; the app keeps only targets the lexicon backs and builds `/lesson?mode=personal` from verified sentences. The guide's pairs become probes, and Strengthen offers "Your plan" for a week. A scripted model covers the path end to end in the tests; a real model has not been run.
- **Thresholds:** a unit is called weak only when its likely range (95% Wilson) is under 85%, and shows that range under twenty answers; a trend needs a difference past chance; a word is listed from its second miss; the guide unlocks at 30 first tries with 5 mistakes and shows the progress.
- **Lexicon, closed-class words:** 30 words that SNK's open-class lists never made rows (nikde, niekde, nikdy, sem, aj, ani, vždy and others) now have Tatoeba sentences (slovak.wiki branch `fix/closed-class-words`). A language review dropped 18 sentences that taught the wrong thing (*Tom šiel niekde inde* after a motion verb, mistranslations, calques), and veď, whose only sentences were a Bible verse and the verb viesť. 27 have three or more usable blanks; nikam, kedysi and nijaký have fewer. Nine of the ten mix-up family pairs drill now (was six); nikam/niekam does not. 13 words found no Tatoeba sentence and stay out. The English glosses of the new rows were written by hand and need a look.
- **CI:** the lint job failed because two components imported types from the server barrel. The types now live in src/core (commit f092dae on fix/learn-from-mistakes-gaps).
- **Verb blanks from UD** (fix/verb-cloze-ud, E28): 161 existing verb sentences gained a blank from the UD Slovak SNK treebank's forms. 28 of the 102 thin verbs now have three or more usable sentences. No sentence text or position changed.
- **Checked:** 10 tests for the analysis and 3 for the saved snapshot and guide; the empty state in the running app. **Unverified:** the page with real answers, and writing a guide with a live model (the call is not covered by a test; a saved guide stands in). The 16 failures in the full suite are the same on main.
