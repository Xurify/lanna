## 1 November: phone and iPad, reachability (second slice)

## 3 November: listening trends (`feat/listening-trends`)

- **Listening trends** (E38, [research/listening-trends.md](research/listening-trends.md)): My mistakes has a Listening section. A typed sentence is compared with what was played and each difference gets a cause (a word not met, vowel length, soft or hushing consonants, voicing, a short word dropped, a word not caught, a different word heard, the wrong ending, i for y, a typing slip), shown per 100 sentences lately against before with "less often / more often / about the same". - **Listen is typing-only.** The Pick / Type switch is gone; every sentence is typed, and "Didn't catch it" is a miss without typing something random. A typed sentence now counts for listening speed, timed by its clip and the plays it took, since the answer time is mostly typing.

- **Things you may not have noticed, and tips:** the Listening section lists findings no single figure shows, only past a 1% test: the ends or starts of sentences lost, a new word costing the words around it, a session that warms up or tires, and words that keep going wrong across sessions with what you typed in their place.
