# LangHarness, November 2026: what was built, when, and what is still unverified

Times are UTC. "Unverified" means the owner has not yet used it in the live app; the sandbox checks and the tests were run in the cloud.

## 2 November: stamps, the minutes ring and the trail log (branch `feat/trail-stamps`)

- **Research:** what gamification does for learning and what harms it, in [research/gamification.md](research/gamification.md); rule E31. The `gamification-loops` agent skill is installed under `.claude/skills/gamification-loops/` with its provenance in `SOURCE.md`.
- **Stamps** (*turistické známky*): a summit stamp per level, a stamp per chapter and 42 deeds (first lesson; 7, 30, 100 and 365 days in a row; 50 to 3,000 words known; 100 to 5,000 review answers; lessons without a miss; words once missed, now known; a balanced week; stories read; scenes said aloud; listening sessions; Blitz scores; hours on the trail; chapter exams passed). Earned by outcomes on record only, dated in `progress.json`, never taken back, no XP. Judged whenever the progress summary is built.
- **Today:** four indicators at the top (days in a row, today's minutes as a ring against the daily goal, words known, XP); a Stamps card in the side column with the last stamp earned and the three closest, each with its count and a progress bar; the This week card counts the days practiced against the goal's days a week and says "Target met" in gold.
- **Lessons:** a stamp earned by the lesson is pressed onto the end screen (a short settle animation, none under reduced motion) with its Slovak name and what it marks.
- **Progress:** a Trail log (hours on the trail, words known, days practiced, longest streak, lessons, review answers, stories read, scenes said aloud) and the Stamp book, in three groups: Summits, Chapters, On the trail.
- **Record:** the day record gains `perfect` (lessons with every answer right); `progress.json` gains `stamps` (stamp id → day earned).
- **Checked in the cloud, on a throwaway data folder:** 17 new tests (the catalog, the earning rule, what is new and what is closest, the longest streak, a lesson minting its stamps end to end and the record keeping them, the disc, the book, the Today card and the week target); typecheck and lint clean; the full suite as before (the one pi-ai package-exports failure under Node 22).
- **Unverified:** the PC, the phone layout of the four indicators on a real device, and which stamps a real learner reaches in the first month.

## 2 November: the trail design (PR #80, merged 11:59Z)

- **Direction:** the app's own look instead of the best-known app's forms (E30, [research/trail-design.md](research/trail-design.md)). Slovak hiking-trail marks for the four kinds of practice (input red, speaking blue, words & rules green, speed yellow), the sun as the one action color (buttons, streak, XP, done), and the path drawn as a climb: levels as stretches of a mountain, chapters as stops on a switchback trail, read from the bottom up, gold behind, dotted ahead.
- **Today:** the streak is a flag and a day count, XP is gold, a ridge line under the greeting shows the whole way to the target level with a marker where the learner stands, every plan task carries its trail mark, the side column shows the current stage of the climb with the current chapter's units under it.
- **Climb page** (`/curriculum`): the whole mountain, each stage with its switchbacks beside its chapters, opened at the learner's stop.
- **Lessons:** the progress bar is a trail of marks, one per question, gold when right, red when missed; the combo is a bolt; the end screen shows a summit, XP in gold, "right" and "days in a row".
- **Navigation:** Today, Climb, Review (due count in gold), Stories, Speak, Words, Progress in the sidebar; Today, Climb, Review, Stories, Progress in the phone tabs; the current door lit by a gold bar, no pill. "Plan" is now called Progress.
- **Type and ground:** Bricolage Grotesque for the chrome; night slate ground `#0f1519` in the window frame, title bar and manifest.
- **Unverified:** the PC, Safari on the phone and iPad, and a light theme (none yet). PR #79's look stays on its branch for comparison.

## 1 November: phone and iPad, first slice

- **Route:** an installable responsive web app served from the PC, not a store app (E20). The reasoning, the iOS limits and the next steps are in [research/mobile-ipad.md](research/mobile-ipad.md).

## 1 November: phone and iPad, reachability (second slice)

- **Route:** Tailscale Serve over the same loopback bind. The server does not listen on the network, even with remote access on, because Serve connects from this computer. Funnel is refused by design (E21). Options and sources: [research/remote-access.md](research/remote-access.md).
- **Switch:** Settings → Devices, off by default. Turning it off signs out every device, and the confirmation says so first.
- **Pairing:** a six-digit code, five minutes, five wrong tries, one code at a time. A paired device stays signed in for 180 days, is listed with its last use, and can be signed out on its own.
- **Gate:** while off, requests a proxy marked, and requests for another host, are refused. While on, the desktop window and paired devices pass; `/pair`, its one POST and the static assets stay public.
- **Recovery:** `npm run remote:off` turns the switch off and signs out every device.
- **Checked in the cloud, on a throwaway data folder:** 35 rule cases, 13 store cases (codes, tries, expiry, sessions, sign-out, turning off, an unreadable file), and a 16-step browser check against a sandbox server: the desktop switch and code, a phone pairing, the paired home screen, and turning it off again.
- **Unverified:** Tailscale and Serve on the owner's PC, and the headers Serve sends (`Host` and the `X-Forwarded-*` headers through Serve are not documented); the iPhone and iPad, Safari's cookie handling, and whether a Home Screen app shares Safari's cookies; the packaged desktop app with remote access on, whose port changes at each start, so it is not yet a fixed Serve target; dev hot reload through a tailnet name, which needs `allowedDevOrigins`; the PC itself, which needs a restart onto main. A Serve setup that sends no identity headers and keeps the Host as loopback would look like this computer; the guide tells the owner to turn the switch on first.
- **Test failures already on main:** 13 tests fail on main without this change: automatic-updates (6), desktop-payload (2), desktop-platform (1), fluency (1), rejected-examples (1), server-title-guard (1) and subscription-auth (1). The next section clears all but one of them.

- **Build warnings, not failures:** Next reports six "dynamic filesystem access" traces (`src/store/graph.ts`, `src/server/watch.ts`, `src/agent/subagents/headless.ts`, `src/agent/tools/pdf-read.ts`). Each can make Next trace more of the project into the server output. Left for a follow-up.

## 1 November: this computer's browser no longer asks for a code

- **Cause:** PR #65 sent every loopback request without a device session to pairing when remote access was on, so the PC's own browser at localhost:3010 got the pairing page (the owner's screenshot).
- **Fix:** a request that names only this computer and carries no proxy evidence gets the same answer as with the switch off: pages pass, and the API passes the desktop check. A request that came through Tailscale Serve still needs a paired device. Proxy evidence: the Tailscale identity headers, `Forwarded`, `X-Real-IP`, or a tailnet name in `Host` or `X-Forwarded-Host`.
- **Checked:** unit tests for the rule (this computer on both names, a phone, every proxy header, a tailnet name through `X-Forwarded-Host`, the desktop API check, and this computer getting the same answer with the switch on or off): 97 tests passing in the four focused files that cover this area (the rules, the store, the desktop API check, and the ChatGPT connection UI). A browser run in the sandbox, 19 checks passing (two runs on fresh profiles; one earlier run failed once at the used-code step, which I could not reproduce): this computer's browser at `localhost` opens the app with the switch on and off; a phone with an identity header goes to pairing, pairs with a code, and is refused again with the switch off.
- **Unverified:** the PC itself (the owner needs to pull main and restart); the packaged Electron window (not launched in the cloud, though its requests take the same path); the Host header Tailscale Serve sends. If Serve rewrites Host to loopback and sends no identity header (tagged devices, Funnel), that traffic now looks like this computer. Keep Funnel off, and check the Host header on the PC.
- **Pairing screen:** the page sat in the app shell's flex row and stayed against the left edge on a desktop. It now takes the full width, and its error line takes no space, so the heading and form are centered on both axes (measured at 1280, 820 and 390 px). The focus ring on its two inputs is 1 px, not the shared 3 px.
- **CI:** this change adds no TypeScript errors. The 89 that had kept main's typecheck and Standalone builds red were cleared by PR #66 (see the typecheck section above).

## 1 November: mix-ups and My mistakes

- **Mix-ups** (`feat/learn-from-mistakes`, E24): a wrong first try that gives another word is kept per pair of words; families and an edit rule predict look-alikes, which are tested as a two-way choice before they are taught. New: the Mix-ups lesson, a Today card, the Strengthen task and a line in the tutor's learner block. English translations are no longer selectable for word lookup in lessons, review, exams, listen and fluency. Research: [research/mixups.md](research/mixups.md).
- **My mistakes** (`feat/learn-from-mistakes`, E25): `/mistakes` shows when your answers were last analyzed, a trend per unit, the words missed most, week by week, and the mix-ups, with an "Analyze now" button and an optional guide on the Cheap slot.
- **Acts on the plan:** a unit that is slipping becomes the Strengthen task; the page has "Do this next" buttons (Mix-ups lesson, a lesson on the weakest unit, add the missed words to review) and an "Add to review" button per word. A lesson renews a snapshot older than six hours.
- **AI, said plainly:** the guide now reads the latest wrong answers and returns patterns with why and a fix, guesses and a plan with buttons. Without a model or a key, the page shows a callout that says what is missing and what AI adds, and opens Settings. "Provider is not configured: anthropic" (a slot set with no key) is now "The anthropic key is missing. Add it in Settings → Keys…". Custom milestones use the same checks.
- **The personal lesson** (E26): the guide runs on the Strong slot and names what to practice; the app keeps only targets the lexicon backs and builds `/lesson?mode=personal` from verified sentences. The guide's pairs become probes, and Strengthen offers "Your plan" for a week. A scripted model covers the path end to end in the tests; a real model has not been run.
- **Thresholds:** a unit is called weak only when its likely range (95% Wilson) is under 85%, and shows that range under twenty answers; a trend needs a difference past chance; a word is listed from its second miss; the guide unlocks at 30 first tries with 5 mistakes and shows the progress.
- **Lexicon, closed-class words:** 30 words that SNK's open-class lists never made rows (nikde, niekde, nikdy, sem, aj, ani, vždy and others) now have Tatoeba sentences (slovak.wiki branch `fix/closed-class-words`). A language review dropped 18 sentences that taught the wrong thing (*Tom šiel niekde inde* after a motion verb, mistranslations, calques), and veď, whose only sentences were a Bible verse and the verb viesť. 27 have three or more usable blanks; nikam, kedysi and nijaký have fewer. Nine of the ten mix-up family pairs drill now (was six); nikam/niekam does not. 13 words found no Tatoeba sentence and stay out. The English glosses of the new rows were written by hand and need a look.
- **CI:** the lint job failed because two components imported types from the server barrel. The types now live in src/core (commit f092dae on fix/learn-from-mistakes-gaps).
- **Verb blanks from UD** (fix/verb-cloze-ud, E28): 161 existing verb sentences gained a blank from the UD Slovak SNK treebank's forms. 28 of the 102 thin verbs now have three or more usable sentences. No sentence text or position changed.
- **Checked:** 10 tests for the analysis and 3 for the saved snapshot and guide; the empty state in the running app. **Unverified:** the page with real answers, and writing a guide with a live model (the call is not covered by a test; a saved guide stands in). The 16 failures in the full suite are the same on main.
