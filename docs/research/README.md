# Research notes

Every task in LangHarness is meant to start from evidence: published language-learning research and the learner's own logs. The notes here say what was read, what was concluded, and what changed in the app. Rule W4 in [../DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md): new research goes here, in Markdown, with sources, and this index is updated.

| Note | Question | Date | What it changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| [comprehensible-input.md](comprehensible-input.md) | Does the app follow comprehensible input, and should it? | 2026-10-28 | Nation's four strands as the frame; the Listen task; meaning-first lesson openers; one task per strand in the daily plan; the strand balance on Today. Not a pure-input app by design. |
| [desirable-difficulties.md](desirable-difficulties.md) | How hard should practice be, and what help is allowed? | 2026-10-28 | Retrieval first everywhere; graded, priced hints; production over recognition; the "strong" stage; Review rating caps; Listen peek rules. |
| [milestones.md](milestones.md) | When will the learner understand a vlog, the news, the in-laws? | 2026-10-28 | The `/milestones` calculator: words (coverage thresholds from Nation, Webb & Rodgers, Adolphs & Schmitt) and speed (parsing, Listen and Watch timing) measured apart. |
| [reading.md](reading.md) | What makes a reading task work? | 2026-10-28 | The Read task: graded episodes at ~97% known words, repeated new words, tap-to-gloss, level-graded question kinds. |
| [speak-fluency.md](speak-fluency.md) | How to build the output and fluency strands, and score pronunciation honestly? | 2026-10-28 | The Speak task (prompts, not recasts), Say it faster and Read it faster, clarity scoring with Azure Speech or transcript matching. |
| [watch.md](watch.md) | How to turn a YouTube video into guided comprehensible input? | 2026-10-28 | The Watch task: line-by-line listening with hidden text, coverage and pre-teach, listening-speed signal. |
| [onboarding.md](onboarding.md) | How should goals and level be found without a long chat? | 2026-10-28 | The four-screen setup, purposes → target, known languages with CEFR levels, two explanation languages, the hours sheet, the placement drill and its limits. |
| [plan-page-ux-audit.md](plan-page-ux-audit.md) | Where should the goal, the hours, the milestones and the re-check live, and why did the goal line read as bad placement? | 2026-10-28 | The Plan page in the sidebar (goal, live pace sliders with Apply, hours, milestone timeline, weak spots, re-check); Today's goal line cut to goal + date + one door; timeline hover chips with full names; real-pace dates gated on seven study days of fourteen. |
| [level-assessment-audit.md](level-assessment-audit.md) | Is the level check buggy or inaccurate? | 2026-10-28 | Found a critical re-check bug, an ETA mismatch, milestones level errors, and construct problems (grammar-only, 8 items per tier, B1 ceiling). Led to the rebuilt adaptive, model-judged check (PR #9, merged) and weak spots on record (PR #11). |
| [slovake-eu-benchmark.md](slovake-eu-benchmark.md) | What should we copy from a free EU-funded course's structure, and what not? | 2026-10-30 | The Talk duplicate and the settings-link bug found while mapping the app (PR #37). The chapter arc, contrast pairs, scripted scenes, exam and "I can" lines, sound track and calendar are proposed in [../plans/chapter-arc.md](../plans/chapter-arc.md), waiting for the owner's decisions. |
| [mobile-ipad.md](mobile-ipad.md) | Should LangHarness become a phone and iPad app, by what route, and what is the first step? | 2026-11-01 | The installable web app over a store wrapper (E20); the first slice: responsive layouts, 44 px touch targets, the drawer sidebar, safe areas, manifest and icons, the press-and-hold microphone and the word-card clamp. Reachability from the device is in [remote-access.md](remote-access.md). |
| [mixups.md](mixups.md) | Are wrong answers kept, and can they find the words a learner confuses, predict similar ones and drill them? | 2026-11-01 | Mix-ups on record per pair of words (E24): first tries that give another word, families and an edit rule that predict look-alikes, a two-way probe before teaching, the Mix-ups lesson, the Today card, Strengthen and the tutor's line. |
| [remote-access.md](remote-access.md) | How can a phone reach the app without the server listening on the network? | 2026-11-01 | Tailscale Serve over the loopback bind, not a LAN bind, Funnel, a relay or a store wrapper; the off-by-default switch, pairing codes, device sessions, and the refusals for proxied and foreign-host requests (E21). |
| [slovak-accuracy-eval.md](slovak-accuracy-eval.md) | How often do Haiku and Sonnet mislead a Slovak learner, at which reasoning level, and does the slovak.wiki skill help? | 2026-11-01 | The skill copied to `.claude/skills/slovak-language` (two fixes not yet in slovak.wiki), the eval in `scripts/eval-slovak-accuracy.ts`, and E27: the rule checker, its note on every practice answer, `check_slovak` and `slovak_reference`, the check-before-you-correct prompt, and the evidence label on every verdict. |
| [trail-design.md](trail-design.md) | What design direction of its own should the app have, keeping what makes a language app work? | 2026-11-02 | The trail design (E30): Slovak trail marks for the four strands, the sun as the one action color, the climb in place of the path, the ridge under the greeting, Bricolage Grotesque for the chrome, seven doors in the sidebar and five phone tabs. |
| [gamification.md](gamification.md) | What should a serious course take from gamification, and which indicators belong at the top of Today? | 2026-11-02 | The stamp book (turistické známky) of outcomes with no XP (E31), the stamp reveal on the lesson end screen, today's minutes ring and words known at the top of Today, the week target, the trail log on Progress, and the `gamification-loops` skill installed under `.claude/skills/`. No leaderboards, hearts or currency, with the reasons. |
| [design-slop.md](design-slop.md) | Which visual defaults make a generated UI look AI-made, and how do agents avoid them? | 2026-11-02 | The `anti-slop-design` skill under `.claude/skills/` with diff checks, the "Design: no AI slop" section in `AGENTS.md`, and E32. |
| [motion.md](motion.md) | Where does the app's motion hurt the feel or the frame rate, and what one system should every screen use? | 2026-11-03 | E35: one timing scale and curve, bars moved by transform, no `transition-all`, press that shrinks, popovers from their trigger, a shake, pop and settle on Review, hover gated to hover devices, reduced motion everywhere. |
| [slow-tabs.md](slow-tabs.md) | Why did clicking between tabs feel very slow, and what fixes it? | 2026-11-03 | E36: run a build (`desktop:prod`) not dev, KaTeX and Markdown out of the shared bundle behind `@/src/core/markdown`, an instant `app/loading.tsx`. Long tasks per page load fell from 620–880 ms to 66–183 ms at 4x CPU. |
| [voice-pace.md](voice-pace.md) | Does "1×" mean one speed, and does the clip metadata agree with the setting? | 2026-11-03 | E37: no. The slovak.wiki voice is generated slow (ElevenLabs 0.88) and its pace varies 2.3 to 5.4 syllables a second by sentence; normalising it at playback failed three times and sounds rushed, so clips play as recorded. `scripts/audio-pace.ts` measures a profile. |
| [listening-trends.md](listening-trends.md) | After a Listen session, can the app say why sentences were missed (vocabulary, hearing, grammar, spelling) and show trends? | 2026-11-03 | E38: typed sentences are aligned with what was played and every difference gets a cause; the trends sit on My mistakes. The Listen route was also dropping `answer`, so typed sentences were logged as picks. |
| [aspect-and-demonstratives.md](aspect-and-demonstratives.md) | Should aspect and demonstratives be practised in tutor-chat units, or in verified drills the app measures? | 2026-11-04 | Built as machinery, inert in the shipped state (E42): an aspect family of 18 verifier-approved rows, with no pair drillable until both aspects have rows (owner sentences, D2); demonstratives as a static grid and an explanation, with no drills yet; the explanation and the cue question after a miss. |
