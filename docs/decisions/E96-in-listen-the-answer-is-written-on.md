# E96. In Listen the answer is written on a line, the top band is the round, and an open cheatsheet is a page of its own (2026-11-09, branch `feat/listen-sheet-redesign`)

**Why:** the owner, on three screenshots of the cheatsheet practice (E95): "It feels kinda too vibe coded and way too generic (every other site type of feeling)", and "Mobile type what you hear feels kinda way too big (oversized) and the placements and elements all feel too awkward". A look at the screens at 390 px found: Listen said the count three times (the bar, "1/10", "Phrase 1 of 10"); the heading and the placeholder were the same words; the answer went into a tinted box with 26 px text; the Show the text control wrapped to a row of its own at the right edge; after an answer the typed text stood in a greyed box right above the same sentence; a card inside a 390 px screen left 302 px for the content; the buttons were 36 px under a finger and showed an Enter key a phone does not have (E4a, E20). On Words, four stat cards and the tabs stood above an open sheet, so on a phone the sheet began a screen and a half down. The end screen said two shares of the sheet as two rows of "N of 180".

**How to apply:**
- **The answer is a line to write on** (`.q-write`, E33), with no box and no placeholder: the heading already says "Type what you hear". After an answer the line is gone and the sentence stands in its place, so the same text is never shown twice. The text shown before an answer (after a second listen, not counted) appears in that same place, above the line.
- **Words are marked where they went wrong** (`heardPieces`, `src/core/listen-analysis.ts`, from the marks of `scoreLine`): a word not typed correctly has a red line under it, a word with only an accent off a teal one, a new word is teal as before. Since E103 a miss is a patch with the typed word under it. Nothing is marked when nothing was typed. The rows of the end screen carry the same marks.
- **The top band is the round** (E88), now shared: `TaskRound`, `Pips` and `Pip` in `components/task-frame.tsx`. Filled teal is typed correctly, pale teal an accent off, a hollow red ring a miss, a hollow grey ring a sentence whose text was shown (since E103 a miss is solid red and a shown sentence a grey slot struck through), grey the one on screen. The same strip sits under the result on both Listen end screens. "Sentence 1 of 10" left the card; the card's head names the source instead (the cheatsheet, the video, or how many new words).
- **On a phone the card is flat** (`PracticeCard phone="flat"`): no surface, no head rule, no inner padding, so the close button, the source, the play button, the line and the dock's buttons share one left edge, and the content is 342 px wide. The play button, the heading and Slowly are one row at every width, as the owner made them in the ear gym on 2026-11-09 ("unify ear gym play and slowly controls on mobile"). Show the text sits under the line and the letter keys: it reads "Show the text" with "after one more listen" beside it until it can be pressed, then "Show the text (not counted)", the owner's wording of the same day. The screen starts at the top, because a phone's keyboard takes the lower half.
- **Under a finger:** Play, Slowly and Show the text keep the focus in the answer line, so a tap on them does not close the keyboard; every button is 44 px tall; the Enter key on Check and Continue is drawn only for a fine pointer (E4a); the keyboard's own key says Done. The X goes back to the cheatsheet, as Escape does (E72).
- **The sheet's result counts what the sheet counts** (E67): "8 of 10 caught by ear", where caught is an answer without the text shown, an accent off included, the same rule as the sheet's progress (E95). The share of the sheet is one bar in three parts (`SheetProgressBar`, `components/words/sheet-progress.tsx`): caught by ear in teal, missed last time as the hollow red ring (solid red since E103), not heard yet in grey, with the three numbers under it. This is the one exception to the `Stats` rows of E77: a share of a whole is drawn as a bar. The open sheet shows the same bar.
- **An open cheatsheet is a page.** The Words title, the counts and the tabs step aside while a sheet is open; "All cheatsheets" goes back to them. The sheet's title is the page title, then what to do with it (**Listen and type** is the one filled button, **Add N words to Review** beside it, the bar under them), then how to look at it (Show all or Cover, the search, Jump to a section), then the phrases. The repo path is shown from 640 px and is the meta line's tooltip below that.
- **Words has no stat cards.** The four counts are one line under the title ("0 can recall at 80%+ · 0 in progress · 0 favorites · 5 saved phrases"); the anti-slop skill names a row of identical cards with a number each. The selected tab is marked by an inset teal line: the border it had was hidden by the app's unlayered `* { border-color }` rule, so no tab looked selected.
- **The language arrives without a mismatch.** `useActiveLanguage` (`components/speech.tsx`) reads the language through `useSyncExternalStore` with an empty server snapshot. Before, a component that hydrated after the language had been fetched began with it while the server HTML had none, and React threw the tree away and drew it again (seen on Listen in 2 of 6 loads): a flash, and the answer line lost its focus.
- **Not changed:** the letter keys (E66), the dock, the other task screens (they keep the card on a phone until someone looks at each), the Words and Saved phrases tabs below the header.
