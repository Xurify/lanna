# E101. Task screens are written on the page, letter keys come in families and fix the letter you just typed, and flags fill their box (2026-11-11, branch `feat/review-on-the-page`)

The owner, after E100: "I was thinking more like Speak, Ear gym, etc", "make the extra letters UX better as well. Maybe develop a faux keyboard or just something with a much better UX and UI", and "Make the flags look better and a bit bigger".

**Why:** every task screen still wrapped its question in the raised practice card with a ruled head, the same box E100 took off Review. The letter keys were seventeen identical keycaps with no grouping and only one action, typing a letter at the caret, so a learner who typed `c` had to delete it to get `č`. Flags were 20 by 13 px on Today and letterboxed inside their box, with a drop shadow.

**How to apply:**
- **`PracticeCard` is flat at every width** (`components/task-frame.tsx`): one quiet head line (icon, title, note, the far-end note) and the question on the page. Lessons, Listen, the ear gym, the chapter exam, Speed, the sound step, the weekly check and Today's line use it. Controls keep their own shapes (answer tiles, keys, buttons), so a control still reads as a control. Listen's phone-only flat card (E96) is now the rule everywhere.
- **The ear gym starts at the top** like the other task screens; E86's "a little above the middle" was for the card and left a loose head line floating once the card was gone.
- **Today's line answers on a writing line**, like Listen (E96); Check waits for typed text.
- **One give-up style:** I didn't catch it and I can't tell are quiet buttons on the left of the dock, as in lessons. Review keeps boxed buttons because each carries its price.
- **Listen's study progress** is the head line's note ("Study day 1 · 0 of 5"), not a boxed strip.
- **Speak:** the scene sheet has no card. On a computer a hairline separates it from the list; on a phone it opens under its row on a hairline. The selected row is a tonal fill.
- **Letter keys come in families** (`components/diacritic-bar.tsx`, logic in `src/core/accent-keys.ts`): one joined keycap group per plain letter (á ä | č | ĺ ľ | ó ô …), 36 px keys (42 px under a finger), so Slovak fits one row on a computer. **The group of the letter before the caret lights up**, and pressing one of its keys changes that letter rather than adding one: `c` then `č` gives `č`; with the caret after the `z` of `zena`, `ž` gives `žena`. The case is kept. It changes the letter only when the result is a different letter, so Estonian `töö` and `jää` can be typed key by key, and never in Cyrillic, where е and ё, и and й are separate letters that follow each other (`её`, `русский`). A group lights up only when one of its keys would change the letter.
- **On a computer the letter keys are a keyboard you open** (owner, 2026-11-11, after four drawn variants: "I like B the most… this will be a desktop feature, not a mobile, because mobile already has a keyboard", and "Not all users will need one"). Where a mouse or trackpad is the main pointer (`pointer: fine`), the letter keys are a Keyboard button (the key left of 1 does the same, on every screen with letter keys) and the note "Hold a letter for its accents". The keyboard is closed by default and its state is kept in this browser (`lh-keyboard`). Open, it is a QWERTY keyboard (ЙЦУКЕН for a Cyrillic language, `components/accent-keyboard.tsx`) whose keys carry their accents as a corner legend; a click types the key's letter, as a real key does, and offers its accents. **Holding a letter on the real keyboard** (the browser's key repeat) opens its accents, numbered; a digit (read by key position, so a Slovak or AZERTY number row works too) turns the letter just typed into that accent and keeps its case; Escape or any other key keeps the plain letter. The numbered accents always appear in the Keyboard line as plain keycaps, open or closed, so nothing floats over the answer and no choice looks like the one Enter would take. This replaces E98's Review-only toggle. Under a finger the grouped pad stays, beside the phone's own keyboard.
- **Flags** (`components/ui/country-flag.tsx`) are cropped to fill their 3:2 box, have a hairline ring and no drop shadow; Today's plan headings use 27 by 18 px flags beside 15 px names.
- **Reviewed** by Fable 5.2 over several rounds; round two found that the first replace rule broke Estonian doubles and Russian `её`.
- **Not changed:** the Today page's cards, the letter strip in chats (the `bar` variant), the jump palette's letters.
