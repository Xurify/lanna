# E103. In Listen a missed word shows what you typed under it, and a missed segment is solid red (2026-11-11, branch `fix/listen-miss-marks`)

The owner, on a screenshot of a missed Listen sentence ("Lekár sa opatrne dotkol zraneného miesta." with a red line under two words and "You typed · Lekár sa opýtrnie dotkol zhraju miesta" in grey under it): "I don't like the way the wrong segments look".

**Why:** the red line said which word was wrong but not how. To see that, the learner had to read the grey line under the sentence and pair its words with the sentence by eye. A new word that was also missed had a red line under teal text, two colors on one word. The grey line was small and low in contrast, and it repeated the whole sentence to show two words.

**How to apply:**
- **A missed word sits on a soft red patch** (`bg-miss-soft`, the same tone as the dock under it) and keeps its own color, so a new word stays teal. What was typed in its place is written small under it in red (a ruby annotation, `ruby-position: under`), as typed, with its case and accents. A word with only an accent off keeps its teal line, with what was typed under it in grey. A skipped word has the patch and nothing under it. Extra typed words are not shown: they are not what was heard.
- **The pairing comes from `scoreLine`** (`src/core/line.ts`): each mark carries `given`, the typed word the alignment set against it. `heardPieces` passes it on.
- **The line height grows only when there is a note**, by the note's own height plus a gap (`calc(1.35em + max(11px, 0.48em) + 6px)`), so on a wrapped sentence a note never runs into the next line's patch, at 30, 24 and 17 px alike.
- **"You typed ·" is gone** from the answer and from the end screen's rows: every typed word is now under the word it was meant to be. A row still says "The text was shown" or "Not caught".
- **A missed segment is solid red** (`bg-miss`, `Pip` in `components/task-frame.tsx`). Then the owner pointed at the round's segment for a miss, the hollow red ring of E88 and E96: "I was talking about this". At 6 px tall, a 1.5 px ring leaves a 3 px slit, so it reads as a heavy outline, not as "missed". Six options were drawn side by side in both themes: the ring, solid red, a thin red line, a wash with a 1 px ring, a grey slot struck through, and solid red at 55%. The agent first picked the struck slot to keep E88's rule that a miss differs in shape as well as color; the owner weighed the 55% red, settled on full solid red ("maybe go with B?") and rejected the rule ("this E88 rule or whatever sucks"). **The shape rule is retired:** a segment's outcome is told apart by color, and no new mark needs a shape of its own. A sentence whose text was shown keeps the grey slot struck through for another reason: a solid grey segment would look like the one for the sentence on screen (`bg-ink-4`). The ear gym's band and the cheatsheet's bar and legend use the same solid red. The weekly check's track (`check-track.tsx`) keeps its grey ring: it is a measurement, and it marks a miss in grey on purpose.
- **Not changed:** Today's line (`components/line/line-page.tsx`) keeps its faded words and its "You typed" line; the weekly check and My mistakes keep theirs.
