# E100. Review is written on the page: no card, a dock for the buttons, and today's answers under the sentence (2026-11-11, branch `feat/review-on-the-page`)

The owner, on two screenshots of Review: "I'm curious what things would look like if we did less of a card UI… Get more creative with it. Don't be afraid to change the layout entirely."

**Why:** the screen was three boxes inside a section: a heading with a rule, a progress bar and a "Still to do" line said the count three ways; then a raised card with its own head (the kind of card, Typing or Choosing, sound) around the sentence; then the buttons, then a footer of Blank and Hide this word. The sentence, which is the task, sat inside the second box. The one rep before it was a single grey row, so the work done today was not on the screen.

**How to apply:**
- **The sentence is on the page.** No card, no card head. The kind of card ("New word 7 of 10", "Relearning", "Review"), where the sentence comes from (from 512 px) and Hide this word are one quiet line above the sentence; Hide this word leaves "Hidden: word, Undo" there until the next answer; the sentence (30 px on a phone, 46 px from 576 px), the translation, then one cell that holds a reserved hint line (the key tip until a hint is taken, then the hints) and the letter keys or the four choices before the answer, and what happened after it, with "you wrote" on the verdict line. From 672 px the cell is at least 112 px tall, so an answer does not move what is under it unless the reason runs long. The play and save buttons beside the translation are held in place before the answer, so the translation wraps the same before and after.
- **The band says the count once.** "Review", a 3 px bar where the heading rule was, "5 of 10 today" ("5 of 10" below 384 px), a settings button and the sound button. "Still to do" is the bar's tooltip and its screen-reader text while a card is on screen, and a sentence on the caught-up screen.
- **The buttons live in a dock** at the foot of the page, the bar the other task screens have (E8, E86): before the answer Show meaning (at most Good, then Show base form, at most Hard, then disabled in the same slot so I don't know never moves) and I don't know (counts as Again), and Check; after it the four ratings as one scale and Continue. The scale is a well with the chosen cell lifted (card surface, teal ring) and each cell's key in its corner; after a miss only Again is possible, so the scale is one line: "Again · comes back in 10 min". The dock is tinted after the answer, teal-wash when right, the miss wash when wrong. On a phone Check and Continue are full width in the same place, and the two branches are keyed so the browser does not reuse a button as another (the reused node counted as a layout shift).
- **The ratings say when the word comes back** in words ("10 min", "1 day", "16 days"), not "10m".
- **Today's answers stay on the page.** Under the sentence, "Answered today": each answer given today, newest first, as ruled lines (the sentence with the card's word underlined teal, pale teal for an accent off, red for a miss; the translation; the rating). The mark beside each line is a filled disc, a pale disc or a hollow ring with a cross, so the three differ by shape; from 576 px the marks hang in the margin, so the lines start on the sentence's edge. The server sends the latest 30 (`answered` on the deck, `ANSWERED_LINES` in `src/server/review.ts`), read from the review log it already walks for the day's count, so the page shows the day after a reload and the caught-up screen ends on the same record (E77). A line answered during this visit fades in.
- **The deck's settings** (Answer by Typing or Choosing, Blank) open from the settings button in the band, as a small panel over the page (32 px targets, 40 px under a finger), so the dock holds only this card's actions and opening them moves nothing.
- **The caught-up screen** is the same page: "All caught up." at the sentence's size (the count is in the band only), the new words per day, the sources, today's answers; Back to Today and Do a lesson in the dock.
- **Not changed:** the grading, the hints and their price, the miss reason, the letter key (E98), the leeches list, the keys (Enter checks and continues, 1 to 4 rate, 1 to 4 pick).
- **Reviewed twice by Fable 5.2** (2026-11-11) before the PR: the moving hint slot, the button heights, the empty dock row in Choosing on a phone, the sunken chosen rating, the six struck-through labels after a miss, the dead space under the key tip, the settings in the dock, and the one-tap Hide with no undo came from that review.
