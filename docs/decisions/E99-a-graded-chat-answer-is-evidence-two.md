# E99. A graded chat answer is evidence: two misses on one unit in three weeks make it a weak spot, and the analysis reads chats beside lessons (2026-11-11, branch `feat/chat-evidence-in-plan`)

**Why:** the owner asked whether the tutor chats decide what to study. They did not. Every `grade` was kept in `chats/answers.jsonl` (E76) and read by nothing but a test, so "perfective future vziať with si" missed in Ask never reached Today, the map, My mistakes or the next day's Strengthen task. The weak-spots record (E1) was fed by the level check, lessons and the review deck only. The owner: "we should offer some detailed analysis and this should weight into what is considered important for me to study in our lesson plans."

**How to apply:**
- **`grade` carries the unit.** An optional `unit` (an id from the course's own list, which the tool's description spells out) says which course unit the question tested. Vocabulary, meaning and open questions leave it out. An unknown id is dropped, never stored (`src/agent/tools/chat.ts`). The record stays free text in `focus`; `unit` is the join to the plan.
- **Grammar in full words (E23).** The unit rows of From your chats carry the unit's `grammarTerm` ("Feminine accusative", "Locative case"), as the curriculum does, not its lesson title ("Objects: -a → -u"). The tutor writes `focus` in full words ("the feminine accusative of this"), never "feminine this"; the tool's description and the chat prompt say so. By unit and the weak-spot lines on Today still show lesson titles, as before.
- **One recorder.** `recordChatAnswer` (`src/server/chat-evidence.ts`) appends the answer, applies it to the weak-spots record and refreshes the chat part of the saved analysis. A failure there is logged and never reaches the chat.
- **The rule** (`noteChatAnswer`, `src/core/weak-spots.ts`): a miss (wrong or "I don't know") flags the unit as a weak spot with source `chat` once chats hold `WEAK_CHAT_MISSES` (2) misses on it within `WEAK_CHAT_DAYS` (21). One wrong answer in a conversation is a slip, the same unit twice is a pattern. Once a spot is on record every chat answer tallies against it: right with no hint is a hit, a miss is a miss, a right answer after a hint is neither (help taken, the first-try rule of E1). The existing mend rule (8 reps since the flag, 85% right) clears it, so chats can also finish a spot.
- **It weighs on the plan through the record that already does.** A `chat` spot sorts after the level check and level with review, before lessons (`weakSpotList`), so the Strengthen task, Today, the map and the tutor's "This learner" block pick it up with no new path. Chats never touch unit accuracy or mastery: those stay lessons' business, because a chat question is looser than a lesson rep.
- **The analysis** (`analyzeChats`, `src/core/chat-analysis.ts`, no model call) reads the answer file the way My mistakes reads lessons: first tries, misses, hinted rights, per unit with Wilson range and trend, per topic (every focus, with what was written and what was wanted, and in how many chats), and the latest misses. It is saved in `analysis.json` as `chat`, retaken with each lesson snapshot and after each graded answer. My mistakes shows **From your chats** (units first, then topics) and says the rule in one line. The guide's facts carry `In chats` lines and the chat answers count toward the 30 first tries it needs.
- **Not changed:** no end-of-chat model call. The evidence is exact at the moment of the answer, so reading it later adds cost and no information. The old `answers.jsonl` rows have no unit and still feed the topic list.
