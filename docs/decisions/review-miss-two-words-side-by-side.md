# A miss is shown as two words side by side, and the miss and right colors say which word a line is about (2026-11-11, branch `fix/review-miss-contrast`)

The owner: "Can we make the explanation area look better? And maybe style it differently", then "I was talking more about the verbose description. It should be more concise", "it doesn't have to be INSANELY concise", and on the dock: "the border should match the card, so it should be red".

**Why:** the area was a stack of sentences ("“Kedy” also means when, but it is another word. This one is keď…"), and an aspect miss ran to five sentences, most of them the general rule. What a learner needs after a miss is a comparison of two words, so the area is built around that one figure ([E100-review-is-written-on-the-page-no](E100-review-is-written-on-the-page-no.md): written on the page, no card).

**How to apply:**
- **The figure** (`components/miss-why.tsx`): "You wrote" and "Needed here" side by side, joined by a hairline: each word in the title font, its meaning under it, and under the needed word the form and the word that decides it ("locative (feminine), after po"). The needed form takes the dictionary's case, so "Keď" at the start of a sentence reads "keď". With no answer, only the needed side shows.
- **One color meaning on the whole card:** the word typed is underlined in the miss red, the word needed in teal, as the sentence above marks them. Every line below that is about one of the two (a reference line, an aspect line, a word in an AI note) carries the same color, as an underline or a short dash, so the eye can match a line to its word. Nothing else is colored.
- **Below a hairline, the why, short:** "Both can mean …" when the two share a meaning, then one explanation (curated rule, aspect pair, reference passage, AI note, or the learner's word in a dictionary sentence) with its source in small grey text. The aspect pair is one line per verb in the curated rule's own words (`roles` in the aspect family of `mixups.json`), not the whole rule. The form is said once, in the column, not again as a sentence. An AI note is asked for in 30 words (it was 45 to 50).
- **The dock** takes the card's state on its top hairline too: a red line after a miss, teal after a right answer, the grey line before the answer. "Again · comes back in 10 min" has the same space on both sides of the dot.
- **Checked** at 1280, 390 and 320 px in Day and Night; "Needed here" is short enough that the two columns stay level at 320 px.
