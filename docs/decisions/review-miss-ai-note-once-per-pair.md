# Where nothing curated or derived explains a pair, an AI note written once from the dictionary's sentences, checked in code and by a judge, cached per pair (2026-11-11, branch `fix/review-miss-contrast`)

**Why:** after [review-miss-explains-from-the-apps-own-data](review-miss-explains-from-the-apps-own-data.md) and [review-miss-reads-the-language-reference](review-miss-reads-the-language-reference.md), 57.5% of the common pairs still had only an example sentence: near-synonyms and derived words (krajina/štát, spoločnosť/firma, veľa/oveľa) that no file in the repo contrasts. Hand-writing 4,773 pairs is not possible, and a model call on every miss makes the card wait about five seconds and pays again for the same pair. A second agent built and graded the model path ([research/miss-note-eval.md](../research/miss-note-eval.md)). On 42 real pairs, the Cheap slot, given only the two glosses and up to five verified sentences per word, wrote 30 notes that passed both checks: 27 right, 2 vague, 1 partly wrong, and no invented form or rule. It said "no clear difference" for 8 pairs, honestly for 4 synonyms. All 6 notes on curated pairs agreed with the hand-written rule. One pair costs about $0.006 to $0.009 and five to seven seconds, once.

**How to apply:**
- **When:** only on a real miss, only when the two words share a meaning and the card has nothing better than an example (`wantsAiNote`, `src/core/review-miss.ts`), each word has at least two verified sentences, and an AI model is set (Settings → AI models). Without one, the card shows the example and nothing breaks.
- **How it arrives:** the card shows at once with "Comparing the two words in the dictionary's sentences…"; the client asks again with `&note=write`, and the note replaces that line and the example. The rating buttons sit in the dock and do not move.
- **Evidence only:** `missNoteBrief` (`src/core/miss-note.ts`) gives glosses, parts of speech and sentences that hold only one of the two words, and asks for "no clear difference" rather than a guess. `verifyMissNote` keeps a note only when every Slovak word in it is in the evidence, both words are named, no grammar category is named that the evidence does not name, and its citations exist. A second call judges it against the same evidence.
- **Cached per pair** ([E1-save-every-generated-artifact-to-data-in](E1-save-every-generated-artifact-to-data-in.md)): `data/profiles/<language>/miss-notes.json`, the reply, kept / none / rejected and why, the judge's verdict, model, cost, time and attempts. A kept note or "no clear difference" is final. A rejected note is written once more on the pair's next miss, then never again (`MISS_NOTE_ATTEMPTS`): in the app, krajina/štát was kept once, rejected on a rewrite for calling "ostrovný" political, and kept on the retry.
- **Labeled:** the note shows its Slovak words in bold and "AI note from the dictionary's sentences, not checked by a person."
- **Measured:** `npm run miss:coverage` counts a pair that would get a note on its first miss. With a model set, 99.6% of the common pairs get a line beyond an example; about one in four of the model's answers is "no clear difference" or rejected, and those keep the example.
- **Not done:** batching the 296 common pairs into a curriculum file the owner reads first (under $2); register and diminutives (dačo, trošku) are out of reach, because the lexicon never names them.
