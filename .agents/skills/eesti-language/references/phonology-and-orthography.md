# Phonology and Orthography (Hääldus ja õigekiri)

Token-minimized reference for pronunciation, sound quantity, palatalization, and spelling rules.

---

## 1. The Vowel System

Standard Estonian has 9 distinctive vowel phonemes:

| Vowel | IPA | Articulation | Approximate Sound / Guidance | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **a** | [ɑ] | Back open unrounded | Like British English _f**a**ther_ | _kala_ (fish) |
| **e** | [e] | Front close-mid unrounded | Like German _B**e**tt_ or Spanish _m**e**_ | _ema_ (mother) |
| **i** | [i] | Front close unrounded | Like English _mach**i**ne_ | _kiri_ (letter) |
| **o** | [o] | Back close-mid rounded | Like German _R**o**se_ | _kodu_ (home) |
| **u** | [u] | Back close rounded | Like English _b**oo**t_ | _kukk_ (rooster) |
| **õ** | [ɤ] | Back close-mid unrounded | High-mid back unrounded. Like Russian _ы_ or Romanian _î_, but produced without rounding lips. | _sõna_ (word), _õpetaja_ (teacher) |
| **ä** | [æ] | Front open unrounded | Open front, identical to English _c**a**t_ | _päev_ (day), _käsi_ (hand) |
| **ö** | [ø] | Front close-mid rounded | Like German _K**ö**nig_ or French _p**eu**_ | _töö_ (work), _öelda_ (to say) |
| **ü** | [y] | Front close rounded | Like German _T**ü**r_ or French _l**u**ne_ | _süsi_ (coal), _üks_ (one) |

_Critical contrast: Never confuse `õ` (back unrounded), `ö` (front rounded), and `o` (back rounded). They create distinct words: `sõda` (war) vs. `söeda` (to coals) vs. `soda` (soda)._

## 2. The Three Quantities (Välted: Q1, Q2, Q3)

A distinguishing feature of Estonian phonology is its three-way phonological contrast in segment and syllable duration:

| Degree | Name | Duration | Orthography | Semantic Minimal Triad (_kabi / kapi / kappi_) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **I välde (Q1)** | Short (_lühike_) | 1 mora (~100–150 ms) | Single letter (_b, d, g_ or single vowel) | _kabi_ (hoof) |
| **II välde (Q2)** | Long (_pikk_) | 2 morae (~200–250 ms) | Single stop _p, t, k_ or double vowel/consonant | _kapi_ (cupboard's — Gen. Sg.) |
| **III välde (Q3)** | Overlong (_ülipikk_) | 3 morae (~350–450 ms) + steep pitch drop | Double stops _pp, tt, kk_ or double vowel/consonant in overlong syllable | _kappi_ (into the cupboard — Ill. Sg.) |

### The Orthographic Overlap (Q2 vs. Q3)

Vowels and geminate sonorants are written identically with two letters in both Q2 and Q3. The distinction must be mastered by morphological role:

- _kooli_ (Q2, Genitive Sg: of the school) vs. _kooli_ (Q3, Short Illative: into the school).
- _linna_ (Q2, Genitive Sg: of the town) vs. _linna_ (Q3, Short Illative: into the town).
- _saada_ (Q2, Imperative: send!) vs. _saada_ (Q3, da-infinitive: to get/receive).

## 3. Consonants & Stop Letter Conventions

Estonian stops are voiceless across all positions. The letters _b, d, g_ do NOT represent voiced English [b, d, g]:

- **b, d, g** = Short voiceless lenis stops ([p̆, t̆, k̆]). They are completely unvoiced, but shorter and weaker than _p, t, k_:
  - _leib_ [leip̆], _tuba_ [tup̆ɑ], _jõgi_ [jɤk̆i].
- **p, t, k** = Long voiceless fortis stops ([pː, tː, kː]):
  - _kapi_ [kɑpːi], _kodu_ vs. _koot_ [koːtː].
- **pp, tt, kk** = Overlong fortis stops ([pːː, tːː, kːː]):
  - _kappi_ [kɑpːːi], _pakk_ [pɑkːː].

## 4. Palatalization (Pehmendus / Palatalisatsioon)

The dental consonants **t, d, s, n, l** become palatalized ([tʲ, dʲ, sʲ, nʲ, lʲ]) in syllables where an _i_ or _j_ historically followed:

- _palk_ [pɑlk] (salary, pay; Gen: _palga_) vs. _palk_ [pɑlʲk] (timber, log; Gen: _palgi_).
- _tall_ [tɑll] (stable) vs. _tall_ [tɑlʲlʲ] (lamb).
- _kott_ [kotʲtʲ] (sack/bag).

_Note: Estonian standard orthography does NOT use an apostrophe or acute accent to mark palatalization in ordinary texts._

## 5. Orthographic Conventions

1. **Lower-Case Norms**:
   - Languages: _eesti keel_, _inglise keel_, _saksa keel_.
   - Nationalities and inhabitants: _eestlane_, _ameeriklane_, _tallinlane_, _tartlane_.
   - Days and months: _esmaspäev_, _reede_, _jaanuar_, _mai_, _september_.
2. **Capitalization**:
   - Proper country and town names: _Eesti_, _Ameerika Ühendriigid_, _Tallinn_, _Tartu_.
   - Official holidays: _jaanipäev_, _jõulud_ (written lowercase!). Only proper personal commemorations are capitalized (_emadepäev_, _iseseisvuspäev_ are lowercase; _vabariigi aastapäev_ is lowercase except for state titles).
3. **Compound Words (Liitsõnad)**:
   - Written as one continuous word when the first element describes the specific kind/type:
     - _kohvitass_ (coffee cup), _arvutilaud_ (computer desk), _sünnipäevapidu_ (birthday party).
   - Hyphenated only when repeating coordinates or technical compounds: _A-kategooria_, _Põhja-Eesti_, _sotsiaal-majanduslik_.
