# Verb Morphology & Conjugation (Tegusõna ja pööramine)

Token-minimized reference for verb principal forms, tenses, moods, voice, and non-finite constructions.

---

## 1. The Four Principal Forms (Põhivormid)

Estonian verb conjugations are derived from four principal stems:

| Verb | *ma*-Infinitive | *da*-Infinitive | 1sg Present (*mina*) | 3sg Past (*tema*) | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *elama* | *elama* | *elada* | *elan* | *elas* | to live |
| *lugema* | *lugema* | *lugeda* | *loen* | *luges* | to read |
| *õppima* | *õppima* | *õppida* | *õpin* | *õppis* | to learn |
| *hakkama* | *hakkama* | *hakata* | *hakkan* | *hakkas* | to begin |
| *minema* | *minema* | *minna* | *lähen* | *läks* | to go |
| *tulema* | *tulema* | *tulla* | *tulen* | *tuli* | to come |
| *olema* | *olema* | *olla* | *olen* | *oli* | to be |
| *tegema* | *tegema* | *teha* | *teen* | *tegi* | to do/make |
| *nägema* | *nägema* | *näha* | *näen* | *nägi* | to see |

## 2. *ma*-Infinitive vs. *da*-Infinitive

The division between *ma* and *da* infinitives is strict and grammaticalized:

### Use the *ma*-Infinitive after:
1. **Motion verbs (destination of action)**: *lähen magama* (I go to sleep), *sõidab töötama* (goes to work).
2. **Action commencement**: *hakkama* (to begin): *hakkab sadama* (it starts raining), *hakkasin õppima* (I started studying).
3. **Duty / Obligation**: *pidama* (must / to have to): *ma pean minema* (I must go).
4. **Invitation / Enticement**: *kutsub sööma* (invites to eat), *õpetab ujuma* (teaches to swim).

### Inflected Forms of the *ma*-Infinitive:
- **-mas** (Inessive, in progress): *Olen söömas.* (I am in the middle of eating.)
- **-mast** (Elative, returning from): *Tulen trennist jooksmast.* (I am coming from running in practice.)
- **-mata** (Abessive, without doing): *Töö jäi tegemata.* (The work remained undone.)
- **-maks** (Translative, purpose): *Seda tehti aitamaks haigeid.* (This was done in order to help the sick.)

### Use the *da*-Infinitive after:
1. **Modal verbs**: *saama* (can/may), *võima* (may/might), *oskama* (know how to), *suutma* (to be able to), *tahtma* (to want), *tohtima* (to be allowed to):
   - *Ma tahan süüa.* (I want to eat.)
   - *Kas ma tohin küsida?* (May I ask?)
   - *Ta oskab eesti keelt rääkida.* (She knows how to speak Estonian.)
2. **Preference / Feeling**: *armastama* (to love), *meeldima* (to please), *kartma* (to fear):
   - *Mulle meeldib suusatada.* (I like to ski.)
3. **Impersonal evaluative phrases**: *on vaja* (it is needed), *on lihtne* (it is easy), *on raske* (it is difficult), *on keelatud* (it is forbidden):
   - *On vaja leiba osta.* (It is necessary to buy bread.)
   - *Siin on keelatud suitsetada.* (Smoking is prohibited here.)

## 3. Tense Conjugations (Indicative Mood)

### Present Tense (Olevik)
Formed on the 1sg present stem (*loen* -> stem *loe-*):

| Person | Ending | Positive | Negative (*ei* + connegative) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1sg (*mina*) | *-n* | *loen* | *ei loe* |
| 2sg (*sina*) | *-d* | *loed* | *ei loe* |
| 3sg (*tema*) | *-b* | *loeb* | *ei loe* |
| 1pl (*meie*) | *-me* | *loeme* | *ei loe* |
| 2pl (*teie*) | *-te* | *loete* | *ei loe* |
| 3pl (*nemad*) | *-vad* | *loevad* | *ei loe* |

### Simple Past Tense (Lihtminevik)
Formed by adding *-s-* or *-i-* to the past stem:
- 1sg: *lugesin* | 1pl: *lugesime* | Negative: *ei lugenud*
- 2sg: *lugesid* | 2pl: *lugesite* | Negative: *ei lugenud*
- 3sg: *luges*   | 3pl: *lugesid*  | Negative: *ei lugenud*

### Compound Past Tenses
- **Perfect (*täisminevik*)**: Present of *olema* + *-nud* participle:
  - *Olen raamatu läbi lugenud.* (I have read the book.)
  - Negation: *Ma ei ole seda näinud.*
- **Pluperfect (*enneminevik*)**: Past of *olema* (*olin/olid/oli...*) + *-nud* participle:
  - *Ta oli juba lahkunud, kui ma saabusin.* (He had already left when I arrived.)

## 4. Moods (Kõneviisid)

### 1. Conditional (Tingiv kõneviis)
Expresses hypothetical actions or polite requests. Suffix is **-ks** appended to the present stem (weak or vocalic stem):
- *olema* -> *oleks*: *Ma oleksin tänulik.* (I would be grateful.)
- *soovima* -> *sooviksin*: *Sooviksin kohvi.* (I would like coffee.)
- *saama* -> *saaksite*: *Kas te saaksite mind aidata?* (Could you help me?)
- Negative: *ei* + *-ks* stem: *Ma ei teeks seda.* (I wouldn't do that.)

### 2. Imperative (Käskiv kõneviis)
Commands and requests:
- **2sg (*sina*)**: Exact connegative stem (no suffix):
  - *Loe!* (Read!), *Tule siia!* (Come here!), *Võta leiba!* (Take some bread!)
  - Negative: **Ära** + stem: *Ära tee!* (Don't do!), *Ära karda!* (Don't be afraid!)
- **2pl (*teie*) / Polite form**: Suffix **-ge** or **-ke** (after consonants):
  - *Lugege!*, *Tulge!*, *Vaadake!*, *Hakake!*
  - Negative: **Ärge** + *-ge/-ke*: *Ärge rääkige!* (Don't speak!), *Ärge unustage!* (Don't forget!)
- **1pl (*meie*)**: *Laskem minna* or colloquial *Lähme! / Hakakem!*
- **3rd Person / Jussive**: Marker **las** or suffix **-gu/-ku**:
  - *Las ta teeb.* (Let him do it.) / *Olgu nii.* (Let it be so.)

### 3. Quotative Mood (Kaudne kõneviis / Evidential)
Used to report statements or rumors without personal commitment to their truth. Suffix is **-vat**:
- *Ta elavat nüüd välismaal.* (He reportedly lives abroad now / People say he lives abroad.)
- *Ilm pidavat homme soojemaks minema.* (The weather is supposed to turn warmer tomorrow.)

---

## 5. Impersonal Voice (Umbisikuline tegumood)

Used when the agent is unspecified or irrelevant (analogous to English passive or German *man*):
- **Present impersonal**: suffix **-takse** (corresponding to *-tud* participle) or **-dakse** (corresponding to *-dud* participle):
  - *Eestis räägitakse eesti keelt.* (In Estonia, Estonian is spoken.)
  - *Õhtuti süüakse sooja toitu.* (In the evenings, warm food is eaten.)
  - Negative: *ei* + stem (*ei räägita, ei tehta, ei sööda*).
- **Past impersonal**: suffix **-ti** or **-di**:
  - *See maja ehitati 2004. aastal.* (This house was built in 2004.)
  - *Tööd tehti hoolega.* (The work was done carefully.)
  - Negative: *ei tehtud, ei räägitud*.
- **Passive Participle**: suffix **-tud** or **-dud**:
  - *Töö on tehtud.* (The work is done.)
  - *Kiri on saadetud.* (The letter has been sent.)

---

## 6. Converb: The *-des* Form

Expresses an action occurring concurrently with the main clause:
- *Lugedes raamatut, kuulas ta muusikat.* (While reading a book, she listened to music.)
- *Tänavat ületades tuleb olla ettevaatlik.* (When crossing the street, one must be careful.)
- Formed by replacing the *da*-infinitive suffix with **-des**: *lugeda -> lugedes*, *teha -> tehes*, *vaadata -> vaadates*.
