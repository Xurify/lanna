# Adjectives, Pronouns, and Numerals

Token-minimized reference for modification, reference, comparison, and quantification.

---

## 1. Adjective Agreement (Omadussõna ühildumine)

In Estonian, attributive adjectives agree with their head noun in **case and number** across cases 1 through 10:
- *suur maja* (a big house) -> *suured majad* (big houses)
- *suure maja* (of a big house) -> *suurte majade* (of big houses)
- *suures majas* (in a big house) -> *suurtes majades* (in big houses)
- *suurele majale* (to a big house) -> *suurtele majadele* (to big houses)

### The 4 Non-Agreeing Ending Cases (-ni, -na, -ta, -ga)
In the last four cases (Terminative, Essive, Abessive, Comitative), adjectives **remain in the Genitive singular or plural** instead of taking the case suffix:

| Case | Ending | Noun with Adjective (Singular) | Noun with Adjective (Plural) | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Terminative** | *-ni* | *suure majani* | *suurte majadeni* | Adjective takes Genitive (*suure*), noun takes *-ni* |
| **Essive** | *-na* | *hea õpetajana* | *heade õpetajatena* | Adjective takes Genitive (*hea*), noun takes *-na* |
| **Abessive** | *-ta* | *külma veeta* | *külmade veteta* | Adjective takes Genitive (*külma*), noun takes *-ta* |
| **Comitative** | *-ga* | *uue autoga* | *uute autodega* | Adjective takes Genitive (*uue*), noun takes *-ga* |

*Warning: Never say `*külmata veeta` or `*uuega autoga` — this is a serious grammatical error.*

## 2. Comparison of Adjectives (Võrdlusastmed)

| Degree | Form | Suffix / Marker | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Positive** (*algvõrre*) | Base adjective | — | *ilus* (beautiful), *suur* (big), *hea* (good) |
| **Comparative** (*keskvõrre*) | Genitive stem + *-m* | *-m* (Gen: *-ma*, Part: *-mat*) | *ilusam, suurem, parem, pikem, vanem* |
| **Superlative** (*ülivõrre*) | 1. Analytic (*kõige* + comp.) | *kõige* + *-m* | *kõige ilusam, kõige suurem, kõige parem* |
| | 2. Synthetic (short form) | *-im* | *suurim, vanim, parim, pikim, uusim* |

Comparative clauses use the particle **kui** ("than") or the elative/partitive:
- *Ta on minust pikem.* (He is taller than me — Elative *minust*).
- *Ta on pikem kui mina.* (He is taller than me — *kui* + Nominative).

## 3. Pronouns (Asesõnad)

### Personal Pronouns (Isikulised asesõnad)
Estonian personal pronouns have full (emphatic) and short (clitic) forms. There is no gender distinction in the 3rd person (*tema / ta* = he/she/it).

| Person | Nom. (Full / Short) | Genitive | Partitive | Allative ("to") | Adessive ("at / have") |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1sg | *mina / ma* | *minu / mu* | *mind* | *mulle* | *mul* |
| 2sg | *sina / sa* | *sinu / su* | *sind* | *sulle* | *sul* |
| 3sg | *tema / ta* | *tema / ta* | *teda* | *talle* | *tal* |
| 1pl | *meie / me* | *meie / me* | *meid* | *meile* | *meil* |
| 2pl | *teie / te* | *teie / te* | *teid* | *teile* | *teil* |
| 3pl | *nemad / nad* | *nende* | *neid* | *neile* | *neil* |

- **Short forms** cannot be used after prepositions or when stressed:
  - *Vaata mind!* (not `*mu`), *minu juures* (not `*mu juures` in formal standard).

### Demonstratives & Relatives
- **See** (this/that, singular): Gen: *selle*, Part: *seda*, Ill: *sellesse / siia*, Iness: *selles / siin*, Elat: *sellest*, All: *sellele*, Adess: *sellel*.
- **Need** (these/those, plural): Gen: *nende*, Part: *neid*, Iness: *nendes*, All: *neile*.
- **Kes** (who — animate): Gen: *kelle*, Part: *keda*, Ill: *kellesse*, Iness: *kelles*, All: *kellele*, Adess: *kellel*.
- **Mis** (what — inanimate): Gen: *mille*, Part: *mida*, Ill: *millesse*, Iness: *milles*, All: *millele*, Adess: *millel*.

## 4. Numerals & Counting (Arvsõnad)

### Counting Rules with Nouns
1. **The number 1 (*üks*)**: Noun stays in **Nominative Singular**:
   - *üks raamat* (one book), *üks poiss* (one boy).
2. **Numbers 2 and above in Nominative**: Head noun is in **Partitive Singular**:
   - *kaks raamatut* (two books)
   - *viis eurot* (five euros)
   - *kümme minutit* (ten minutes)
   - *sada aastat* (a hundred years)
3. **Compound Numbers**: The final element dictates the form:
   - *kakskümmend üks aastat* (21 years: *kakskümmend üks* + Partitive *aastat* or Nom *aasta*).
4. **Declined Numerals (Oblique Cases)**: When the numeral is in an oblique case, **both the numeral and noun decline together**:
   - Inessive: *kahes suures linnas* (in two big cities — both in Inessive sg).
   - Allative: *kolmele lapsele* (to three children — both in Allative sg).
   - Comitative: *viie sõbraga* (with five friends — numeral Genitive *viie*, noun Comitative *sõbraga*).

### Clock Time & Calendar
- **Exact hour**: *Kell on kolm.* (It is three o'clock.)
- **Half past**: *Kell on pool neli.* (It is 3:30 — literally "half of the fourth").
- **Quarter past**: *Kell on veerand neli.* (It is 3:15 — "quarter of the fourth").
- **Quarter to**: *Kell on kolmveerand neli.* (It is 3:45 — "three quarters of the fourth").
- **Minutes**: *Kümme minutit neli läbi* (4:10); *Kümne minuti pärast viis* (4:50).
- **Days & Dates**: *Esmaspäeval* (on Monday), *teisipäeval* (on Tuesday).
  - *viiendal mail* (on the fifth of May — Ordinal in Adessive).
