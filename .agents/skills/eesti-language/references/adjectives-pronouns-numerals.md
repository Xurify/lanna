# Adjectives, Pronouns, and Numerals

Token-minimized reference for modification, reference, comparison, and quantification.

---

## 1. Adjective Agreement (Omadussõna ühildumine)

In Estonian, attributive adjectives agree with their head noun in **case and number** across cases 1 through 10:

- _suur maja_ (a big house) -> _suured majad_ (big houses)
- _suure maja_ (of a big house) -> _suurte majade_ (of big houses)
- _suures majas_ (in a big house) -> _suurtes majades_ (in big houses)
- _suurele majale_ (to a big house) -> _suurtele majadele_ (to big houses)

### The 4 Non-Agreeing Ending Cases (-ni, -na, -ta, -ga)

In the last four cases (Terminative, Essive, Abessive, Comitative), adjectives **remain in the Genitive singular or plural** instead of taking the case suffix:

| Case | Ending | Noun with Adjective (Singular) | Noun with Adjective (Plural) | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Terminative** | _-ni_ | _suure majani_ | _suurte majadeni_ | Adjective takes Genitive (_suure_), noun takes _-ni_ |
| **Essive** | _-na_ | _hea õpetajana_ | _heade õpetajatena_ | Adjective takes Genitive (_hea_), noun takes _-na_ |
| **Abessive** | _-ta_ | _külma veeta_ | _külmade veteta_ | Adjective takes Genitive (_külma_), noun takes _-ta_ |
| **Comitative** | _-ga_ | _uue autoga_ | _uute autodega_ | Adjective takes Genitive (_uue_), noun takes _-ga_ |

_Warning: Never say `*külmata veeta` or `*uuega autoga` — this is a serious grammatical error._

## 2. Comparison of Adjectives (Võrdlusastmed)

| Degree | Form | Suffix / Marker | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Positive** (_algvõrre_) | Base adjective | — | _ilus_ (beautiful), _suur_ (big), _hea_ (good) |
| **Comparative** (_keskvõrre_) | Genitive stem + _-m_ | _-m_ (Gen: _-ma_, Part: _-mat_) | _ilusam, suurem, parem, pikem, vanem_ |
| **Superlative** (_ülivõrre_) | 1. Analytic (_kõige_ + comp.) | _kõige_ + _-m_ | _kõige ilusam, kõige suurem, kõige parem_ |
|  | 2. Synthetic (short form) | _-im_ | _suurim, vanim, parim, pikim, uusim_ |

Comparative clauses use the particle **kui** ("than") or the elative/partitive:

- _Ta on minust pikem._ (He is taller than me — Elative _minust_).
- _Ta on pikem kui mina._ (He is taller than me — _kui_ + Nominative).

## 3. Pronouns (Asesõnad)

### Personal Pronouns (Isikulised asesõnad)

Estonian personal pronouns have full (emphatic) and short (clitic) forms. There is no gender distinction in the 3rd person (_tema / ta_ = he/she/it).

| Person | Nom. (Full / Short) | Genitive | Partitive | Allative ("to") | Adessive ("at / have") |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1sg | _mina / ma_ | _minu / mu_ | _mind_ | _mulle_ | _mul_ |
| 2sg | _sina / sa_ | _sinu / su_ | _sind_ | _sulle_ | _sul_ |
| 3sg | _tema / ta_ | _tema / ta_ | _teda_ | _talle_ | _tal_ |
| 1pl | _meie / me_ | _meie / me_ | _meid_ | _meile_ | _meil_ |
| 2pl | _teie / te_ | _teie / te_ | _teid_ | _teile_ | _teil_ |
| 3pl | _nemad / nad_ | _nende_ | _neid_ | _neile_ | _neil_ |

- **Short forms** cannot be used after prepositions or when stressed:
  - _Vaata mind!_ (not `*mu`), _minu juures_ (not `*mu juures` in formal standard).

### Demonstratives & Relatives

- **See** (this/that, singular): Gen: _selle_, Part: _seda_, Ill: _sellesse / siia_, Iness: _selles / siin_, Elat: _sellest_, All: _sellele_, Adess: _sellel_.
- **Need** (these/those, plural): Gen: _nende_, Part: _neid_, Iness: _nendes_, All: _neile_.
- **Kes** (who — animate): Gen: _kelle_, Part: _keda_, Ill: _kellesse_, Iness: _kelles_, All: _kellele_, Adess: _kellel_.
- **Mis** (what — inanimate): Gen: _mille_, Part: _mida_, Ill: _millesse_, Iness: _milles_, All: _millele_, Adess: _millel_.

## 4. Numerals & Counting (Arvsõnad)

### Counting Rules with Nouns

1. **The number 1 (_üks_)**: Noun stays in **Nominative Singular**:
   - _üks raamat_ (one book), _üks poiss_ (one boy).
2. **Numbers 2 and above in Nominative**: Head noun is in **Partitive Singular**:
   - _kaks raamatut_ (two books)
   - _viis eurot_ (five euros)
   - _kümme minutit_ (ten minutes)
   - _sada aastat_ (a hundred years)
3. **Compound Numbers**: The final element dictates the form:
   - _kakskümmend üks aastat_ (21 years: _kakskümmend üks_ + Partitive _aastat_ or Nom _aasta_).
4. **Declined Numerals (Oblique Cases)**: When the numeral is in an oblique case, **both the numeral and noun decline together**:
   - Inessive: _kahes suures linnas_ (in two big cities — both in Inessive sg).
   - Allative: _kolmele lapsele_ (to three children — both in Allative sg).
   - Comitative: _viie sõbraga_ (with five friends — numeral Genitive _viie_, noun Comitative _sõbraga_).

### Clock Time & Calendar

- **Exact hour**: _Kell on kolm._ (It is three o'clock.)
- **Half past**: _Kell on pool neli._ (It is 3:30 — literally "half of the fourth").
- **Quarter past**: _Kell on veerand neli._ (It is 3:15 — "quarter of the fourth").
- **Quarter to**: _Kell on kolmveerand neli._ (It is 3:45 — "three quarters of the fourth").
- **Minutes**: _Kümme minutit neli läbi_ (4:10); _Kümne minuti pärast viis_ (4:50).
- **Days & Dates**: _Esmaspäeval_ (on Monday), _teisipäeval_ (on Tuesday).
  - _viiendal mail_ (on the fifth of May — Ordinal in Adessive).
