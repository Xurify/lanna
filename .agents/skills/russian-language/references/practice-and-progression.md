# Russian Practice, Assessment, and Progression

Curriculum authoring and pedagogical progression must align with the official State Standards for Russian as a Foreign Language (ТРКИ / TORFL).

## 1. The TORFL / ТРКИ Level Framework

| ТРКИ Level | CEFR Equiv. | Active Lexicon | Grammatical Benchmark Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Элементарный (ТЭУ)** | **A1** | ~780 words | All 6 cases in basic singular meanings; basic plural nominative/accusative; present, past, future; imperative; motion verbs *идти/ходить, ехать/ездить*; *по-, при-*; short adjectives *рад, должен, занят, болен*; *у меня есть/нет*. |
| **Базовый (ТБУ)** | **A2** | ~1300 words | Plurals across all cases; comparatives and superlatives; motion verbs with *в-, вы-, у-, под-, от-, до-, пере-*; *лететь, нести, везти*; conditional with *если бы*; indirect question with *ли*; telephone discourse. |
| **Первый (ТРКИ-1)** | **B1** | ~2300 words | All 4 participles (active/passive, present/past); short passive participles; verbal adverbs (*деепричастия*); passive with `-ся`; complex clauses of concession, cause, result; abstract vocabulary. |
| **Второй (ТРКИ-2)** | **B2** | 5000+ words | Full stylistic range; official documents (*заявление, доверенность, резюме*); all 7 intonational constructions (ИК); nuanced modal and expressive idioms; high conversational fluency. |

## 2. Diagnostic Error Taxonomy

When analyzing student errors in drills, categorize by root linguistic mechanism:

| Code | Error Category | Manifestation | Remediation Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ERR-CASE-GOV** | Preposition / Verb Valency | Using wrong case after preposition or verb (*помогать + acc* instead of *dat*). | Isolate the governing head word; show the minimal pair with required case question (*кому?*). |
| **ERR-ANIM** | Animacy Failure | Using nominative instead of genitive for masculine animate object (*я видел студент*). | Check animacy hierarchy: person/animal + accusative = genitive ending. |
| **ERR-PAUCAL** | Numeral Agreement | Using nominative plural after 2, 3, 4 (*два столы* instead of *два стола*). | Reinforce the historical dual rule: 2, 3, 4 take Genitive Singular in nominative contexts. |
| **ERR-ASPECT** | Aspect Confusion | Using imperfective for completed single event or perfective in present tense. | Contrast process vs result; test with time markers (*долго, часто* vs *вдруг, за час*). |
| **ERR-MOTION** | Motion Verb Misdirection | Using *идти* for habitual trips or for vehicle travel. | Contrast foot (*идти/ходить*) vs vehicle (*ехать/ездить*); single trip (*идти*) vs round trip (*ходить*). |
| **ERR-SPELL-7** | 7-Letter Rule Breach | Writing *ы* after *г, к, х, ж, ч, ш, щ* (*книгы*). | Enforce standard orthographic filter: write *и*, never *ы*. |
| **ERR-VOWEL-RED** | Vowel Reduction Error | Spelling words phonetically based on sound (*спаси́бо* written *спаси́ба*). | Highlight base dictionary stem and stress position (*аканье* in pronunciation, etymological *о* in writing). |

## 3. Curriculum and Drill Authoring Rules

When writing drills, cloze items, or assessment cards for Russian:

1. **Unambiguous Context:** A fill-in-the-blank item must contain sufficient syntactic cues (preposition, governing verb, or subject agreement) so that exactly one form is grammatically correct.
   - Poor: *Я вижу ___ (сестра).* (Both *сестру* and *сестёр* could be valid without context).
   - Good: *Я вижу мою младшую ___ (сестра).* (Governs *сестру* uniquely).
2. **Prepositions Inside the Blank:** If a preposition has mobile forms (*в/во, с/со, к/ко, о/об/обо*), include the preposition inside the cloze token if the target word dictates the variant:
   - Example: *Он подошёл ___ (к + я) на улице.* → Target: *ко мне*.
3. **Cyrillic Script Only:** Never present Russian exercises in Latin transliteration. Transliteration masks palatalization and orthographic rules.
4. **Mark Stress Pedagogically:** In prompts and explanations for A1–B1 learners, always place combining acute stress marks (U+0301) on words of two or more syllables, and preserve `ё`.
5. **Authentic Tatoeba Sentences:** Ensure example frames reflect genuine native collocations rather than calqued English syntax.
