# Russian Participles and Gerunds (Причастия и Деепричастия)

Participles (*причастия*) and verbal adverbs / gerunds (*деепричастия*) combine verbal semantics (tense, aspect, transitivity) with adjectival and adverbial syntax. They are characteristic of formal, literary, academic, and journalistic registers (TRKI B1–B2).

## 1. Participles (*Причастия*)

Participles function as verbal adjectives, agreeing with their head noun in gender, number, and case. There are four full participles plus short-form passive participles:

| Participle Type | Aspect | Time Reference | Suffixes | Formation Base | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active Present** | Imperfective | Contemporary with main clause | `-ущ- / -ющ-` (1st conj)<br>`-ащ- / -ящ-` (2nd conj) | 3pl present stem (`чита-ют`, `говор-ят`) | *чита́ющий, говоря́щий* (reading, speaking) |
| **Active Past** | Both (usually СВ) | Preceding main clause | `-вш-` (vowel stems)<br>`-ш-` (consonant stems) | Past stem (`прочита-л`, `нёс`) | *прочита́вший, нёсший* (having read, having carried) |
| **Passive Present** | Imperfective | Contemporary with main clause | `-ем- / -ом-` (1st conj)<br>`-им-` (2nd conj) | 1pl present stem (`чита-ем`, `люб-им`) | *чита́емый, люби́мый* (being read, beloved) |
| **Passive Past** | Perfective | Preceding main clause | `-нн-` (stems in `-ать/-ять`)<br>`-енн- / -ённ-` (others)<br>`-т-` (monosyllabic / `-оть/-уть`) | Infinitive / Past stem (`написа-л`, `откры-л`) | *напи́санный, ку́пленный, откры́тый* (written, bought, opened) |

### Participle Agreement and Clauses
Participle phrases (*причастные обороты*) following their noun must be separated by commas:
- *Студент, **читающий книгу**, учится в нашем университете.* (The student reading the book studies at our university.)
- *Я получил письмо, **написанное другом**.* (I received a letter written by a friend.)
If placed before the noun, no commas are used:
- *Я получил **написанное другом** письмо.*

### Short Passive Participles (*Краткие страдательные причастия*)
Function as passive predicates, inflecting for gender and number with a single `-н-`:
- Masculine: `-н / -т` (*напи́сан, откры́т*)
- Feminine: `-на / -та` (*напи́сана, откры́та*)
- Neuter: `-но / -то` (*напи́сано, откры́то*)
- Plural: `-ны / -ты` (*напи́саны, откры́ты*)

*Agent in Passive Construction:* The agent performing the action appears in the bare **Instrumental case**:
- *Письмо написано моим братом.* (The letter was written by my brother.)
- *Дверь открыта ветром.* (The door was opened by the wind.)

## 2. Verbal Adverbs / Gerunds (*Деепричастия*)

Verbal adverbs modify verbs, expressing an accompanying action performed by the **same subject**. They are indeclinable (never change endings).

| Type | Aspect | Temporal Meaning | Formation Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Imperfective Gerund** | Imperfective | Simultaneous action (*while doing*) | Present stem + `-а / -я` | *чита́я* (reading, while reading)<br>*говоря́* (speaking)<br>*бу́дучи* (being — from *быть*) |
| **Perfective Gerund** | Perfective | Preceding completed action (*having done*) | Infinitive / Past stem + `-в / -вши / -ши` | *прочита́в* (having read)<br>*уви́дев* (having seen)<br>*принеся́* (having brought) |

### Reflexive Gerunds
Reflexive gerunds always take `-сь`:
- *улыба́ясь* (smiling — imperfective)
- *верну́вшись* (having returned — perfective).

### Subject Constraint Rule (Absolute Syntactic Law)
The action of the verbal adverb must be executed by the grammatical subject of the main clause:
- **Correct:** *Возвращаясь домой, **я** встретил друга.* (Returning home, I met a friend.)
- **Incorrect:** \**Возвращаясь домой, у меня упала шапка.* (Unacceptable dangling modifier, because the hat didn't return home).

In colloquial speech, Russian speakers replace participle and gerund clauses with subordinate clauses introduced by *который* or *когда / в то время как*:
- Literary: *Человек, сидевший у окна, читал газету.*
- Spoken: *Человек, который сидел у окна, читал газету.*
