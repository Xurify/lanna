# Russian Participles and Gerunds (Причастия и Деепричастия)

Participles (_причастия_) and verbal adverbs / gerunds (_деепричастия_) combine verbal semantics (tense, aspect, transitivity) with adjectival and adverbial syntax. They are characteristic of formal, literary, academic, and journalistic registers (TRKI B1–B2).

## 1. Participles (_Причастия_)

Participles function as verbal adjectives, agreeing with their head noun in gender, number, and case. There are four full participles plus short-form passive participles:

| Participle Type | Aspect | Time Reference | Suffixes | Formation Base | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active Present** | Imperfective | Contemporary with main clause | `-ущ- / -ющ-` (1st conj)<br>`-ащ- / -ящ-` (2nd conj) | 3pl present stem (`чита-ют`, `говор-ят`) | _чита́ющий, говоря́щий_ (reading, speaking) |
| **Active Past** | Both (usually СВ) | Preceding main clause | `-вш-` (vowel stems)<br>`-ш-` (consonant stems) | Past stem (`прочита-л`, `нёс`) | _прочита́вший, нёсший_ (having read, having carried) |
| **Passive Present** | Imperfective | Contemporary with main clause | `-ем- / -ом-` (1st conj)<br>`-им-` (2nd conj) | 1pl present stem (`чита-ем`, `люб-им`) | _чита́емый, люби́мый_ (being read, beloved) |
| **Passive Past** | Perfective | Preceding main clause | `-нн-` (stems in `-ать/-ять`)<br>`-енн- / -ённ-` (others)<br>`-т-` (monosyllabic / `-оть/-уть`) | Infinitive / Past stem (`написа-л`, `откры-л`) | _напи́санный, ку́пленный, откры́тый_ (written, bought, opened) |

### Participle Agreement and Clauses

Participle phrases (_причастные обороты_) following their noun must be separated by commas:

- _Студент, **читающий книгу**, учится в нашем университете._ (The student reading the book studies at our university.)
- _Я получил письмо, **написанное другом**._ (I received a letter written by a friend.)
  If placed before the noun, no commas are used:
- _Я получил **написанное другом** письмо._

### Short Passive Participles (_Краткие страдательные причастия_)

Function as passive predicates, inflecting for gender and number with a single `-н-`:

- Masculine: `-н / -т` (_напи́сан, откры́т_)
- Feminine: `-на / -та` (_напи́сана, откры́та_)
- Neuter: `-но / -то` (_напи́сано, откры́то_)
- Plural: `-ны / -ты` (_напи́саны, откры́ты_)

_Agent in Passive Construction:_ The agent performing the action appears in the bare **Instrumental case**:

- _Письмо написано моим братом._ (The letter was written by my brother.)
- _Дверь открыта ветром._ (The door was opened by the wind.)

## 2. Verbal Adverbs / Gerunds (_Деепричастия_)

Verbal adverbs modify verbs, expressing an accompanying action performed by the **same subject**. They are indeclinable (never change endings).

| Type | Aspect | Temporal Meaning | Formation Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Imperfective Gerund** | Imperfective | Simultaneous action (_while doing_) | Present stem + `-а / -я` | _чита́я_ (reading, while reading)<br>_говоря́_ (speaking)<br>_бу́дучи_ (being — from _быть_) |
| **Perfective Gerund** | Perfective | Preceding completed action (_having done_) | Infinitive / Past stem + `-в / -вши / -ши` | _прочита́в_ (having read)<br>_уви́дев_ (having seen)<br>_принеся́_ (having brought) |

### Reflexive Gerunds

Reflexive gerunds always take `-сь`:

- _улыба́ясь_ (smiling — imperfective)
- _верну́вшись_ (having returned — perfective).

### Subject Constraint Rule (Absolute Syntactic Law)

The action of the verbal adverb must be executed by the grammatical subject of the main clause:

- **Correct:** _Возвращаясь домой, **я** встретил друга._ (Returning home, I met a friend.)
- **Incorrect:** \*_Возвращаясь домой, у меня упала шапка._ (Unacceptable dangling modifier, because the hat didn't return home).

In colloquial speech, Russian speakers replace participle and gerund clauses with subordinate clauses introduced by _который_ or _когда / в то время как_:

- Literary: _Человек, сидевший у окна, читал газету._
- Spoken: _Человек, который сидел у окна, читал газету._
