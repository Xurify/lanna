# Russian Communication, Etiquette, and Vocabulary

Social interaction in Russian requires navigating register, naming formulas, polite distance, and avoiding critical false friends.

## 1. The T–V Distinction: *Ты* vs. *Вы*

Russian distinguishes informal singular *ты* from formal/plural *вы*:

| Address | Target & Context | Etiquette Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Вы** (Formal singular) | Adult strangers, service personnel, teachers, doctors, business associates, acquaintances | The default polite address. In formal correspondence addressed to one specific individual, capitalized as **Вы / Ваш**. |
| **Вы** (Plural) | Any group of two or more people (regardless of familiarity) | Always takes plural verb and adjective agreement (*Вы готовы?*). |
| **Ты** (Informal singular) | Family members, close friends, peers/classmates among youth, children under ~14 | Never address an adult stranger as *ты*. Transitioning from *вы* to *ты* (*перейти на «ты»*) requires mutual agreement. |

## 2. Eastern Slavic Naming Conventions

A full Russian name consists of three elements: **Имя (Given Name) + Отчество (Patronymic) + Фамилия (Surname)**.

| Component | Example | Formation & Meaning | Context of Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **First Name + Patronymic** | *Александр Сергеевич, Анна Ивановна* | Patronymic formed from father's name: `-ович / -евич` (masc), `-овна / -евна` (fem). | The highest standard of respectful address in business, education, government, and medicine. |
| **Hypocoristic / Short Name** | *Саша (Александр), Маша (Мария), Ваня (Иван)* | Formed with `-а/-я`. | Used between friends, family, peers with *ты*. |
| **Diminutive / Endearing** | *Сашенька, Машенька, Ванюша, котик* | Formed with diminutive suffixes `-еньк-, -ушк-, -ик-`. | Intimate affection, parents to children. |
| **Surname Alone** | *Иванов, Смирнова* | Typically `-ов/-ова, -ин/-ина, -ский/-ская`. | Official rosters, police, roll calls. |

## 3. High-Value Situational Formulas

| Function | Formal / Standard | Informal / Friendly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Greeting** | *Здравствуйте! Добрый день! Доброе утро! Добрый вечер!* | *Привет! Здорово!* |
| **Farewell** | *До свидания! Всего доброго! Всего хорошего!* | *Пока! До скорого! До завтра!* |
| **Thanks** | *Большое спасибо. Благодарю вас.* | *Спасибо! Спасибочки!* |
| **Responding to thanks** | *Пожалуйста. Не за что. На здоровье.* | *Не за что! Да ладно!* |
| **Apology** | *Извините. Прошу прощения. Извините за беспокойство.* | *Прости. Извини.* |
| **Agreeing / Accepting** | *Конечно. Согласен / Согласна. С удовольствием.* | *Давай! Договорились! Ладно!* |
| **Refusing politely** | *К сожалению, не могу. Боюсь, что не получится.* | *Не, не выйдет.* |
| **Asking to repeat** | *Повторите, пожалуйста. Я не расслышал(а).* | *Что? Как ты сказал?* |

## 4. Crucial False Friends (*Ложные друзья переводчика*)

Cross-linguistic interference causes severe errors if assumptions from English or other Slavic languages are applied blindly:

| Russian Word | True Meaning in Russian | What Learners Mistake It For (Trap) | True Russian for the Mistaken Word |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **чёрствый** | stale (bread), unfeeling | fresh (Slovak *čerstvý*) | *свежий* |
| **овощи** | vegetables | fruit (Slovak *ovocie*) | *фрукты* |
| **позор** | shame, disgrace | attention (Slovak *pozor*) | *внимание* |
| **неделя** | week | Sunday (Slovak *nedeľa*) | *воскресенье* |
| **красный** | red | beautiful (Slovak *krásny*, archaic Rus.) | *красивый* |
| **родина** | homeland / motherland | family (Slovak *rodina*) | *семья* |
| **живот** | belly / stomach | life (Slovak *život*) | *жизнь* |
| **магазин** | shop / store | periodical magazine | *журнал* |
| **город** | city / town | castle (Slovak *hrad*) | *за́мок / крепость* |
| **заказать** | to order (food, goods) | to forbid (Slovak *zakázať*) | *запретить* |
| **аккуратный** | neat, tidy, punctilious | accurate (English accurate) | *точный, правильный* |
| **фамилия** | surname / last name | family (English family) | *семья* |
| **артист** | performer / actor | painter / fine artist | *художник* |
| **кабинет** | private office / study / treatment room | kitchen cupboard / cabinet | *шкаф / шкафчик* |
