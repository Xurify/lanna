# Verb Morphology & Conjugation (Tegusõna ja pööramine)

Token-minimized reference for verb principal forms, tenses, moods, voice, and non-finite constructions.

---

## 1. The Four Principal Forms (Põhivormid)

Estonian verb conjugations are derived from four principal stems:

| Verb      | _ma_-Infinitive | _da_-Infinitive | 1sg Present (_mina_) | 3sg Past (_tema_) | Meaning    |
| --------- | --------------- | --------------- | -------------------- | ----------------- | ---------- |
| _elama_   | _elama_         | _elada_         | _elan_               | _elas_            | to live    |
| _lugema_  | _lugema_        | _lugeda_        | _loen_               | _luges_           | to read    |
| _õppima_  | _õppima_        | _õppida_        | _õpin_               | _õppis_           | to learn   |
| _hakkama_ | _hakkama_       | _hakata_        | _hakkan_             | _hakkas_          | to begin   |
| _minema_  | _minema_        | _minna_         | _lähen_              | _läks_            | to go      |
| _tulema_  | _tulema_        | _tulla_         | _tulen_              | _tuli_            | to come    |
| _olema_   | _olema_         | _olla_          | _olen_               | _oli_             | to be      |
| _tegema_  | _tegema_        | _teha_          | _teen_               | _tegi_            | to do/make |
| _nägema_  | _nägema_        | _näha_          | _näen_               | _nägi_            | to see     |

---

## 2. _ma_-Infinitive vs. _da_-Infinitive

The division between _ma_ and _da_ infinitives is strict and grammaticalized:

### Use the _ma_-Infinitive after:

1. **Motion verbs (destination of action)**: _lähen magama_ (I go to sleep), _sõidab töötama_ (goes to work).
2. **Action commencement**: _hakkama_ (to begin): _hakkab sadama_ (it starts raining), _hakkasin õppima_ (I started studying).
3. **Duty / Obligation**: _pidama_ (must / to have to): _ma pean minema_ (I must go).
4. **Invitation / Enticement**: _kutsub sööma_ (invites to eat), _õpetab ujuma_ (teaches to swim).

### Inflected Forms of the _ma_-Infinitive:

- **-mas** (Inessive, in progress): _Olen söömas._ (I am in the middle of eating.)
- **-mast** (Elative, returning from): _Tulen trennist jooksmast._ (I am coming from running in practice.)
- **-mata** (Abessive, without doing): _Töö jäi tegemata._ (The work remained undone.)
- **-maks** (Translative, purpose): _Seda tehti aitamaks haigeid._ (This was done in order to help the sick.)

### Use the _da_-Infinitive after:

1. **Modal verbs**: _saama_ (can/may), _võima_ (may/might), _oskama_ (know how to), _suutma_ (to be able to), _tahtma_ (to want), _tohtima_ (to be allowed to):
   - _Ma tahan süüa._ (I want to eat.)
   - _Kas ma tohin küsida?_ (May I ask?)
   - _Ta oskab eesti keelt rääkida._ (She knows how to speak Estonian.)
2. **Preference / Feeling**: _armastama_ (to love), _meeldima_ (to please), _kartma_ (to fear):
   - _Mulle meeldib suusatada._ (I like to ski.)
3. **Impersonal evaluative phrases**: _on vaja_ (it is needed), _on lihtne_ (it is easy), _on raske_ (it is difficult), _on keelatud_ (it is forbidden):
   - _On vaja leiba osta._ (It is necessary to buy bread.)
   - _Siin on keelatud suitsetada._ (Smoking is prohibited here.)

## 3. Tense Conjugations (Indicative Mood)

### Present Tense (Olevik)

Formed on the 1sg present stem (_loen_ -> stem _loe-_):

| Person        | Ending | Positive | Negative (_ei_ + connegative) |
| ------------- | ------ | -------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1sg (_mina_)  | _-n_   | _loen_   | _ei loe_                      |
| 2sg (_sina_)  | _-d_   | _loed_   | _ei loe_                      |
| 3sg (_tema_)  | _-b_   | _loeb_   | _ei loe_                      |
| 1pl (_meie_)  | _-me_  | _loeme_  | _ei loe_                      |
| 2pl (_teie_)  | _-te_  | _loete_  | _ei loe_                      |
| 3pl (_nemad_) | _-vad_ | _loevad_ | _ei loe_                      |

### Simple Past Tense (Lihtminevik)

Formed by adding _-s-_ or _-i-_ to the past stem:

- 1sg: _lugesin_ | 1pl: _lugesime_ | Negative: _ei lugenud_
- 2sg: _lugesid_ | 2pl: _lugesite_ | Negative: _ei lugenud_
- 3sg: _luges_ | 3pl: _lugesid_ | Negative: _ei lugenud_

### Compound Past Tenses

- **Perfect (_täisminevik_)**: Present of _olema_ + _-nud_ participle:
  - _Olen raamatu läbi lugenud._ (I have read the book.)
  - Negation: _Ma ei ole seda näinud._
- **Pluperfect (_enneminevik_)**: Past of _olema_ (_olin/olid/oli..._) + _-nud_ participle:
  - _Ta oli juba lahkunud, kui ma saabusin._ (He had already left when I arrived.)

## 4. Moods (Kõneviisid)

### 1. Conditional (Tingiv kõneviis)

Expresses hypothetical actions or polite requests. Suffix is **-ks** appended to the present stem (weak or vocalic stem):

- _olema_ -> _oleks_: _Ma oleksin tänulik._ (I would be grateful.)
- _soovima_ -> _sooviksin_: _Sooviksin kohvi._ (I would like coffee.)
- _saama_ -> _saaksite_: _Kas te saaksite mind aidata?_ (Could you help me?)
- Negative: _ei_ + _-ks_ stem: _Ma ei teeks seda._ (I wouldn't do that.)

### 2. Imperative (Käskiv kõneviis)

Commands and requests:

- **2sg (_sina_)**: Exact connegative stem (no suffix):
  - _Loe!_ (Read!), _Tule siia!_ (Come here!), _Võta leiba!_ (Take some bread!)
  - Negative: **Ära** + stem: _Ära tee!_ (Don't do!), _Ära karda!_ (Don't be afraid!)
- **2pl (_teie_) / Polite form**: Suffix **-ge** or **-ke** (after consonants):
  - _Lugege!_, _Tulge!_, _Vaadake!_, _Hakake!_
  - Negative: **Ärge** + _-ge/-ke_: _Ärge rääkige!_ (Don't speak!), _Ärge unustage!_ (Don't forget!)
- **1pl (_meie_)**: _Laskem minna_ or colloquial _Lähme! / Hakakem!_
- **3rd Person / Jussive**: Marker **las** or suffix **-gu/-ku**:
  - _Las ta teeb._ (Let him do it.) / _Olgu nii._ (Let it be so.)

### 3. Quotative Mood (Kaudne kõneviis / Evidential)

Used to report statements or rumors without personal commitment to their truth. Suffix is **-vat**:

- _Ta elavat nüüd välismaal._ (He reportedly lives abroad now / People say he lives abroad.)
- _Ilm pidavat homme soojemaks minema._ (The weather is supposed to turn warmer tomorrow.)

---

## 5. Impersonal Voice (Umbisikuline tegumood)

Used when the agent is unspecified or irrelevant (analogous to English passive or German _man_):

- **Present impersonal**: suffix **-takse** (corresponding to _-tud_ participle) or **-dakse** (corresponding to _-dud_ participle):
  - _Eestis räägitakse eesti keelt._ (In Estonia, Estonian is spoken.)
  - _Õhtuti süüakse sooja toitu._ (In the evenings, warm food is eaten.)
  - Negative: _ei_ + stem (_ei räägita, ei tehta, ei sööda_).
- **Past impersonal**: suffix **-ti** or **-di**:
  - _See maja ehitati 2004. aastal._ (This house was built in 2004.)
  - _Tööd tehti hoolega._ (The work was done carefully.)
  - Negative: _ei tehtud, ei räägitud_.
- **Passive Participle**: suffix **-tud** or **-dud**:
  - _Töö on tehtud._ (The work is done.)
  - _Kiri on saadetud._ (The letter has been sent.)

---

## 6. Converb: The _-des_ Form

Expresses an action occurring concurrently with the main clause:

- _Lugedes raamatut, kuulas ta muusikat._ (While reading a book, she listened to music.)
- _Tänavat ületades tuleb olla ettevaatlik._ (When crossing the street, one must be careful.)
- Formed by replacing the _da_-infinitive suffix with **-des**: _lugeda -> lugedes_, _teha -> tehes_, _vaadata -> vaadates_.
