# Grade Alternation (Astmevaheldus)

Grade alternation (*astmevaheldus*) is the core morphophonological process of Estonian. It governs the alternation between a **strong grade** (*tugev aste*) and a **weak grade** (*nõrk aste*) in the root syllable of inflected nouns and verbs.

---

## 1. The Two Main Types

### 1. Quantitative Alternation (Kvantitatiivne astmevaheldus)
Only the length (quantity) of consonants or vowels changes, but the sound quality remains the same:
- **pp -> p**: *sepp* (Q3, smith) : *sepa* (Q2) : *seppa* (Q3)
- **tt -> t**: *kott* (bag) : *koti* : *kotti*; *võtma* (to take) : *võtan*
- **kk -> k**: *pakk* (package) : *paki* : *pakki*; *hakkama* : *hakkab* (Q3 vs Q2)
- **ss -> s**: *poiss* (boy) : *poisi* : *poissi*
- **Length reduction (Q3 -> Q2)** without consonant spelling change:
  - *linn* (Q3, city) : *linna* (Q2) : *linna* (Q3)
  - *kool* (Q3, school) : *kooli* (Q2) : *kooli* (Q3)
  - *laulma* (Q3, to sing) : *laulan* (Q2)

### 2. Qualitative Alternation (Kvalitatiivne astmevaheldus)
A consonant changes into another consonant or disappears completely (*kaob*):
- **b -> v**: *tuba* : *toa* (historical *b* dropped); *leib* : *leiva*
- **d -> j**: *rada* : *raja* (path); *sada* : *saja* (hundred)
- **d -> Ø**: *riid* : *riiu* (quarrel); *vedama* : *vean* (to pull); *tegema* : *teen* (to do)
- **g -> Ø**: *jõgi* : *jõe* (river); *nuga* : *noa* (knife); *lugu* : *loo* (story); *lugema* : *loen* (to read)
- **k -> Ø**: *tõbi* : *tõve*
- **ht -> h**: *leht* : *lehe* (leaf/sheet); *täht* : *tähe* (star/letter)
- **sk -> s**: *usk* : *usu* (faith/belief); *viskama* : *viskan* (to throw)
- **lk -> l**: *jalg* : *jala* (foot/leg); *palk* : *palga* (salary)
- **rk -> r**: *hark* : *hargu* (fork); *mürk* : *mürgi* (poison)

---

## 2. Direction of Alternation

Alternation operates in two opposite directions across vocabulary classes:

### Direction A: Weakening Alternation (Nõrgenev astmevaheldus)
The dictionary form (Nominative Sg or *ma*-infinitive) is in the **strong grade**, and the Genitive Sg or Present stem is in the **weak grade**.

| Word Class | Strong Grade | Weak Grade | Strong Grade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nouns** | **Nominative Sg** | **Genitive Sg** | **Partitive Sg** |
| *tuba* (room) | *tuba* (strong) | *toa* (weak) | *tuba* (strong) |
| *leib* (bread) | *leib* (strong) | *leiva* (weak) | *leiba* (strong) |
| *sepp* (smith) | *sepp* (strong) | *sepa* (weak) | *seppa* (strong) |
| *jõgi* (river) | *jõgi* (strong) | *jõe* (weak) | *jõge* (strong) |
| **Verbs** | ***ma*-infinitive** | **1sg Present (*mina*)** | **3sg Past (*tema*)** |
| *lugema* (to read) | *lugema* (strong) | *loen* (weak) | *luges* (strong) |
| *teadma* (to know) | *teadma* (strong) | *tean* (weak) | *teadis* (strong) |
| *õppima* (to learn) | *õppima* (strong) | *õpin* (weak) | *õppis* (strong) |

### Direction B: Strengthening Alternation (Tugevnev astmevaheldus)
The dictionary form is in the **weak grade**, while the Genitive Sg or Present stem is in the **strong grade**. Common in words ending in a consonant or *-e*.

| Word Class | Weak Grade | Strong Grade | Weak Grade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nouns** | **Nominative Sg** | **Genitive Sg** | **Partitive Sg** |
| *mõte* (thought) | *mõte* (weak) | *mõtte* (strong) | *mõtet* (weak) |
| *hammas* (tooth) | *hammas* (weak) | *hamba* (strong) | *hammast* (weak) |
| *kallis* (expensive/dear) | *kallis* (weak) | *kalli* (strong) | *kallist* (weak) |
| *kingitus* (gift) | *kingitus* (weak) | *kingituse* (strong) | *kingitust* (weak) |
| *tütar* (daughter) | *tütar* (weak) | *tütre* (strong) | *tütart* (weak) |
| **Verbs** | ***ma*-infinitive** | **1sg Present (*mina*)** | ***da*-infinitive** |
| *hakkama* (to begin) | *hakkama* (strong) | *hakkan* (strong) | *hakata* (weak) |
| *tõusma* (to rise) | *tõusma* (strong) | *tõusen* (weak) | *tõusta* (strong) |
| *pöörduma* (to turn) | *pöörduma* (strong) | *pöördun* (strong) | *pöörduda* (weak) |

---

## 3. Non-Alternating Words (Astmevahelduseta sõnad)

A large portion of the vocabulary does not undergo grade alternation:
- Words without stop consonants (*k, p, t, b, d, g, s*) in the alternation position: *kala : kala : kala*, *kana : kana : kana*, *elu : elu : elu*.
- Most recent foreign loanwords: *auto : auto : autot*, *arvuti : arvuti : arvutit*, *kohv : kohvi : kohvi*, *buss : bussi : bussi*.
- Derived nouns ending in *-us, -lane, -line, -mine*: *laulmine : laulmise*, *eestlane : eestlase*.

---

## 4. Practical Rule for Learners

When encountering a new noun or verb:
1. Always learn the **three base forms** together (*tuba, toa, tuba*).
2. If form 1 and form 2 have different consonant quantities or vowels, identify whether it is weakening (*pp -> p, g -> Ø*) or strengthening (*t -> tt, mmas -> mba*).
3. The singular cases 4–14 always follow the **Genitive** grade:
   - Weak genitive *toa* -> *toas, toast, toale, toal* (all weak).
   - Strong genitive *mõtte* -> *mõttes, mõttest, mõttele* (all strong).
