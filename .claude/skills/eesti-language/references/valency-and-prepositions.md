# Valency, Prepositions, and Postpositions (Rektsioon ja kaassõnad)

Token-minimized reference for verb government (*rektsioon*) and the adpositional system.

---

## 1. The Adpositional System (Ees- ja tagasõnad)

Estonian is predominantly **postpositional** (*tagasõnad*), placing the adposition after a noun in the **Genitive** case. A smaller set of **prepositions** (*eessõnad*) precedes a noun in the **Partitive** or other cases.

### Postpositions Governing the Genitive (Tagasõnad + omastav)

Most spatial postpositions form their own 3-way directional triad:

| Base Concept | Motion Towards (*Kuhu?*) | Static Location (*Kus?*) | Motion Away (*Kust?*) | Example with Genitive *laua* ("table") |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **On top / Surface** | *peale* | *peal* | *pealt* | *laua peale / laua peal / laua pealt* |
| **Underneath** | *alla* | *all* | *alt* | *laua alla / laua all / laua alt* |
| **In front** | *ette* | *ees* | *eest* | *maja ette / maja ees / maja eest* |
| **Behind** | *taha* | *taga* | *tagant* | *maja taha / maja taga / maja tagant* |
| **At / Beside (person)** | *juurde* | *juures* | *juurest* | *arsti juurde / arsti juures / arsti juurest* |
| **Beside / Side** | *kõrvale* | *kõrval* | *kõrvalt* | *tee kõrvale / tee kõrval / tee kõrvalt* |
| **Inside** | *sisse* | *sees* | *seest* | *kasti sisse / kasti sees / kasti seest* |
| **Between** | *vahele* | *vahel* | *vahelt* | *kahe maja vahele / vahel / vahelt* |
| **Around** | *ümber* | *ümber* | *ümbert* | *linna ümber* (around the city) |

Non-spatial postpositions governing Genitive:
- **jaoks**: *Minu jaoks on see oluline.* (For me, this is important.)
- **pärast**: *Pärast tööd.* (After work.) / *Sinu pärast.* (Because of you.)
- **tõttu**: *Vihma tõttu jäi mäng ära.* (Due to rain, the game was cancelled.)
- **kohta**: *Raamat Eesti ajaloo kohta.* (A book about Estonian history.)
- **eest**: *Tänan abi eest!* (Thank you for the help!)
- **kestel / jooksul**: *Aasta jooksul.* (During the course of a year.)

### Prepositions Governing the Partitive (Eessõnad + osastav)
Prepositions precede the noun and typically demand the **Partitive**:
- **ilma**: *ilma suhkruta* (Abessive) or *ilma suhkrut* (Partitive) — "without sugar".
- **enne**: *enne lõunat* (before lunch), *enne kella kuut* (before six o'clock).
- **mööda**: *kõndis mööda teed* (walked along the road).
- **läbi**: *sõitis läbi linna* (drove through the city).
- **piki**: *piki kallast* (along the shore).
- **keset**: *keset tuba* (in the middle of the room).
- **vastu**: *vastu seina* (against the wall); *vastu ööd* (towards night).
- **vaatamata**: *vaatamata halvale ilmale* (despite bad weather — governs Allative).

### Prepositions with Other Cases
- **kuni** + **Terminative** (*-ni*): *kuni homseni* (until tomorrow), *kuni jaamani* (up to the station).
- **alates** + **Elative** (*-st*): *alates esmaspäevast* (starting from Monday).

## 2. Verb Valency Catalogue (Tegusõnade rektsioon)

Learners frequently make transfer errors from English or Russian prepositions. Use this verified catalog:

### 1. Verbs Requiring Partitive (*Keda? Mida?*)
- *aitama*: *Aita mind!* (Help me! — NEVER `*Aita mulle`).
- *ootama*: *Ootame rongi.* (We are waiting for the train — no preposition).
- *otsima*: *Otsin võtit.* (I am looking for a key).
- *segama*: *See müra segab mind.* (This noise disturbs me).
- *tänama*: *Tänan teid südamest.* (I thank you from the heart).
- *kartma*: *Kardan pimedust.* (I fear the dark).
- *armastama*: *Armastan muusikat.* (I love music).
- *kuulama*: *Kuulan õpetajat.* (I listen to the teacher).
- *vaatama*: *Vaatan filmi.* (I watch the movie).

### 2. Verbs Requiring Allative (*Kellele? Millele?*)
Used for recipients, experiencers, and indirect targets:
- *meeldima*: *Mulle meeldib see raamat.* (I like this book — literally "To me pleases this book").
- *maitsema*: *Supp maitseb mulle väga.* (The soup tastes very good to me).
- *tunduma*: *See tundub mulle imelik.* (That seems strange to me).
- *helistama*: *Helistan arstile.* (I call the doctor).
- *ütlema / rääkima*: *Ütle mulle tõtt.* (Tell me the truth).
- *kinkima*: *Kingin emale lilli.* (I give flowers to mother).
- *lootma*: *Loodan sinule.* (I rely / count on you).

### 3. Verbs Requiring Adessive (*Kellel? Millel?*)
Possession and physical/emotional states:
- *olema*: *Mul on külm.* (I am cold.) / *Mul on aega.* (I have time.)
- *puuduma*: *Mul puudub kogemus.* (I lack experience.)
- *õnnestuma*: *Tal õnnestus eksam sooritada.* (He succeeded in passing the exam.)
- *külas käima*: *Käisin sõbral külas.* (I visited a friend.)

### 4. Verbs Requiring Elative (*Kellest? Millest?*)
Topics, origins, and psychological causes:
- *rääkima / vestlema*: *Räägime tööst.* (We talk about work.)
- *mõtlema*: *Mõtlen sageli sinust.* (I often think of you.)
- *kirjutama*: *Autor kirjutab loodusest.* (The author writes about nature.)
- *sõltuma / olenema*: *Kõik sõltub ilmast.* (Everything depends on the weather.)
- *hoolima*: *Ta ei hooli rahast.* (He doesn't care about money.)
- *loobuma*: *Loobusin plaanist.* (I gave up the plan.)
- *huvituma*: *Huvitun ajaloost.* (I am interested in history.)

### 5. Verbs Requiring Illative (*Kellesse? Millesse?*)
Internal investment, faith, and relationship:
- *armuma*: *Armusin temasse.* (I fell in love with her/him.)
- *uskuma*: *Usun tulevikku.* (I believe in the future.)
- *suhtuma*: *Kuidas sa temasse suhtud?* (How do you feel toward him/her?)
- *puutuma*: *See ei puutu asjasse.* (That is beside the point / doesn't concern this.)
