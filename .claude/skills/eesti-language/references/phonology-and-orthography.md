# Phonology and Orthography (Hääldus ja õigekiri)

Token-minimized reference for pronunciation, sound quantity, palatalization, and spelling rules.

---

## 1. The Vowel System

Standard Estonian has 9 distinctive vowel phonemes:

| Vowel | IPA | Articulation | Approximate Sound / Guidance | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **a** | [ɑ] | Back open unrounded | Like British English *f**a**ther* | *kala* (fish) |
| **e** | [e] | Front close-mid unrounded | Like German *B**e**tt* or Spanish *m**e*** | *ema* (mother) |
| **i** | [i] | Front close unrounded | Like English *mach**i**ne* | *kiri* (letter) |
| **o** | [o] | Back close-mid rounded | Like German *R**o**se* | *kodu* (home) |
| **u** | [u] | Back close rounded | Like English *b**oo**t* | *kukk* (rooster) |
| **õ** | [ɤ] | Back close-mid unrounded | High-mid back unrounded. Like Russian *ы* or Romanian *î*, but produced without rounding lips. | *sõna* (word), *õpetaja* (teacher) |
| **ä** | [æ] | Front open unrounded | Open front, identical to English *c**a**t* | *päev* (day), *käsi* (hand) |
| **ö** | [ø] | Front close-mid rounded | Like German *K**ö**nig* or French *p**eu*** | *töö* (work), *öelda* (to say) |
| **ü** | [y] | Front close rounded | Like German *T**ü**r* or French *l**u**ne* | *süsi* (coal), *üks* (one) |

*Critical contrast: Never confuse `õ` (back unrounded), `ö` (front rounded), and `o` (back rounded). They create distinct words: `sõda` (war) vs. `söeda` (to coals) vs. `soda` (soda).*

## 2. The Three Quantities (Välted: Q1, Q2, Q3)

A distinguishing feature of Estonian phonology is its three-way phonological contrast in segment and syllable duration:

| Degree | Name | Duration | Orthography | Semantic Minimal Triad (*kabi / kapi / kappi*) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **I välde (Q1)** | Short (*lühike*) | 1 mora (~100–150 ms) | Single letter (*b, d, g* or single vowel) | *kabi* (hoof) |
| **II välde (Q2)** | Long (*pikk*) | 2 morae (~200–250 ms) | Single stop *p, t, k* or double vowel/consonant | *kapi* (cupboard's — Gen. Sg.) |
| **III välde (Q3)** | Overlong (*ülipikk*) | 3 morae (~350–450 ms) + steep pitch drop | Double stops *pp, tt, kk* or double vowel/consonant in overlong syllable | *kappi* (into the cupboard — Ill. Sg.) |

### The Orthographic Overlap (Q2 vs. Q3)
Vowels and geminate sonorants are written identically with two letters in both Q2 and Q3. The distinction must be mastered by morphological role:
- *kooli* (Q2, Genitive Sg: of the school) vs. *kooli* (Q3, Short Illative: into the school).
- *linna* (Q2, Genitive Sg: of the town) vs. *linna* (Q3, Short Illative: into the town).
- *saada* (Q2, Imperative: send!) vs. *saada* (Q3, da-infinitive: to get/receive).

## 3. Consonants & Stop Letter Conventions

Estonian stops are voiceless across all positions. The letters *b, d, g* do NOT represent voiced English [b, d, g]:

- **b, d, g** = Short voiceless lenis stops ([p̆, t̆, k̆]). They are completely unvoiced, but shorter and weaker than *p, t, k*:
  - *leib* [leip̆], *tuba* [tup̆ɑ], *jõgi* [jɤk̆i].
- **p, t, k** = Long voiceless fortis stops ([pː, tː, kː]):
  - *kapi* [kɑpːi], *kodu* vs. *koot* [koːtː].
- **pp, tt, kk** = Overlong fortis stops ([pːː, tːː, kːː]):
  - *kappi* [kɑpːːi], *pakk* [pɑkːː].

## 4. Palatalization (Pehmendus / Palatalisatsioon)

The dental consonants **t, d, s, n, l** become palatalized ([tʲ, dʲ, sʲ, nʲ, lʲ]) in syllables where an *i* or *j* historically followed:
- *palk* [pɑlk] (salary, pay; Gen: *palga*) vs. *palk* [pɑlʲk] (timber, log; Gen: *palgi*).
- *tall* [tɑll] (stable) vs. *tall* [tɑlʲlʲ] (lamb).
- *kott* [kotʲtʲ] (sack/bag).

*Note: Estonian standard orthography does NOT use an apostrophe or acute accent to mark palatalization in ordinary texts.*

## 5. Orthographic Conventions

1. **Lower-Case Norms**:
   - Languages: *eesti keel*, *inglise keel*, *saksa keel*.
   - Nationalities and inhabitants: *eestlane*, *ameeriklane*, *tallinlane*, *tartlane*.
   - Days and months: *esmaspäev*, *reede*, *jaanuar*, *mai*, *september*.
2. **Capitalization**:
   - Proper country and town names: *Eesti*, *Ameerika Ühendriigid*, *Tallinn*, *Tartu*.
   - Official holidays: *jaanipäev*, *jõulud* (written lowercase!). Only proper personal commemorations are capitalized (*emadepäev*, *iseseisvuspäev* are lowercase; *vabariigi aastapäev* is lowercase except for state titles).
3. **Compound Words (Liitsõnad)**:
   - Written as one continuous word when the first element describes the specific kind/type:
     - *kohvitass* (coffee cup), *arvutilaud* (computer desk), *sünnipäevapidu* (birthday party).
   - Hyphenated only when repeating coordinates or technical compounds: *A-kategooria*, *Põhja-Eesti*, *sotsiaal-majanduslik*.
