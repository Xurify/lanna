# Source

Written for LangHarness on 2026-11-02 from Developers Digest, "AI design slop and how to spot it" (https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/ai-design-slop-and-how-to-spot-it). The article lists the visual tells of AI-generated pages and measures them with sixteen deterministic DOM and CSS checks. The skill keeps its list of tells and its main remedies (one layout primitive repeated, one bold choice, contrast that passes AA, customized shadcn tokens, design decisions written down), and maps each one to the trail design rules (E4, E4a, E30, E31) in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. The grep checks in `SKILL.md` are this repo's own, not the article's.
